This page retells only registries and the bank's own documents. Press reports about the owner are not carried over.

## Ownership chain

- **ALTA banka a.d. Beograd** (registration number 07074433, formerly JUBMES banka, ISIN RSJUBME48945): 2,211,690 shares.
- 100% of the shares belong to **ALTA GROUP doo Beograd** (registration number 21035254).
- According to the CRHoV shareholder register as of 30.10.2026, the National Bank's register of banks, the Business Registers Agency (APR) records and the bank's own shareholder meeting minutes, ALTA GROUP belongs to a single owner, Davor Macura. These sources show no other shareholders.
- The bank owns ALTA Leasing doo and ALTA banka AD Bitola (North Macedonia).

## Board (Upravni odbor)

According to the bank's website and the National Bank's register of banks as of 31.10.2026: Davor Macura is chairman; independent members are Aleksandar Živković, Kristijan Ristić (since 29.04.2026), Nebojša Stojanović and Vladimir Miladinović.

## Executive board (Izvršni odbor)

According to the bank's website: Una Sikimić (chair), Nikola Krdžić, Tatjana Kuljak, Bojan Pantović, Željko Devčić.

## Capital increases by the owner

According to the bank's shareholder meeting minutes, the sole shareholder, ALTA GROUP, recapitalised the bank several times:

- 18.08.2025: 266,817 new shares;
- 24.12.2025: 232,446 shares, 2.35 billion dinars, all bought by ALTA GROUP;
- January to April 2026: 232,531 shares, about 2.35 billion dinars;
- 19.06.2026: 290,538 shares, 2.93 billion dinars, all bought by ALTA GROUP.

This is the owner's money, not depositors'. It does not affect the payout of up to €50,000.

## What the registries do not show

- The composition of the management bodies is not in the annual statements exported from APR: it is taken from the bank's website and the National Bank's register.
- The dates of the National Bank's consent to board appointments are not available.
- Which proceedings the National Bank has started against shareholders and the board, it has not specified ([statement of 31.10.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21851)).
