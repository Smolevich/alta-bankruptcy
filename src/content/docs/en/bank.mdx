This page uses only public registries, the bank's own documents and official National Bank announcements. We leave out press reports about the owner.

<h2 id="why">Why the licence was revoked, in the National Bank's words</h2>

Verbatim from the National Bank's announcement, without our interpretation:

> “The Executive Board noted that the delicensing was preceded by supervisory activities aimed at enabling the bank to increase its liquidity levels. It was established that the bank’s financial condition deteriorated significantly in the prior period and that there is a likelihood of further deterioration of its financial condition or liquidity, thereby threatening the interests of the bank’s depositors.” — [National Bank of Serbia, English version, 31.10.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21850); [Serbian original, 30.10.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21849).

## Ownership chain

- **ALTA banka a.d. Beograd** (registration number 07074433, formerly JUBMES banka, ISIN RSJUBME48945): 2,211,690 shares.
- 100% of the shares belong to **ALTA GROUP doo Beograd** (registration number 21035254).
- According to the CRHoV shareholder register as of 30.10.2026, the National Bank's register of banks and the bank's shareholder meeting minutes, the bank's sole shareholder is ALTA GROUP. According to APR records, ALTA GROUP has a single owner, Davor Macura. These sources show no other shareholders.
- The bank owns ALTA Leasing doo and ALTA banka AD Bitola (North Macedonia).

## Board (Upravni odbor)

<p class="bank-note">Composition as of 31.10.2026. Since 03.11.2026 the bank has been run by AOD as bankruptcy administrator: the APR register lists AOD as the bank's legal representative (section «Законски заступници»).</p>

According to the bank's website and the National Bank's register of banks as of 31.10.2026: Davor Macura is chairman; independent members are Aleksandar Živković, Kristijan Ristić (since 29.04.2026), Nebojša Stojanović and Vladimir Miladinović.

## Executive board (Izvršni odbor)

<p class="bank-note">Composition as of 31.10.2026. Since 03.11.2026 the bank has been run by AOD as bankruptcy administrator: the APR register lists AOD as the bank's legal representative (section «Законски заступници»).</p>

According to the bank's website: Una Sikimić (chair), Nikola Krdžić, Tatjana Kuljak, Bojan Pantović, Željko Devčić.

## Capital increases by the owner

According to the bank's shareholder meeting minutes, the sole shareholder, ALTA GROUP, recapitalised the bank several times:

- 18.08.2025: 266,817 new shares;
- 24.12.2025: 232,446 shares, 2.35 billion dinars, all bought by ALTA GROUP;
- January to April 2026: 232,531 shares, about 2.35 billion dinars;
- 19.06.2026: 290,538 shares, 2.93 billion dinars, all bought by ALTA GROUP.

This is the owner's money. It does not affect the insured payout of up to €50,000. In bankruptcy, capital contributions are returned only from what is left after all creditors are paid ([art. 20 item 7 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)).

## What the registries do not show

- The APR annual statements do not say who sits on the boards; we took that from the bank's website and the National Bank's register.
- The dates of the National Bank's consent to board appointments are not available.
- The National Bank has not said what proceedings it has started against shareholders, management bodies and other responsible persons ([statement of 31.10.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21851)).
