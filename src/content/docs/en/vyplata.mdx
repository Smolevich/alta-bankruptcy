AOD pays up to €50,000 through the paying bank, with no court involved. Anything above that is claimed separately in court: see [«Claim above €50,000»](/en/trebovanie/).

## When: you can collect the insured amount from 13.11.2026 until 03.11.2029

On 06.11.2026 AOD officially announced the date ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)):

- **The insured amount is paid from 13.11.2026** through Banka Poštanska štedionica (the paying bank). The last day is **03.11.2029**: three years from the court decision opening bankruptcy ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)).
- **Amount: up to €50,000 per depositor.** Dinar deposits are paid in dinars, foreign-currency deposits in euros (see [«How much and in which currency»](#how-much)).
- **Who:** individuals (residents and non-residents), sole traders (preduzetnici), micro, small and medium-sized legal entities registered in Serbia. Details: [«Who is entitled to the payout»](#who-is-eligible).
- **Where:** at the Poštanska branches on AOD's list of 06.11.2026. The list has 54 branches in 13 cities. One of them, in Belgrade (Kraljice Marije 3), is marked “specialised, for payout only” (SPECIJALIZOVANA EKSPOZITURA SAMO ZA ISPLATU). What this mark means is not clear from the AOD document. Addresses and hours are [below](#postanska-branches) ([AOD list, PDF](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Spisak.pdf)). Per AOD, addresses and hours are also available at any Poštanska branch.
- **What to bring:** [checklist below](#what-to-bring).

**Individual depositor (collecting for yourself):**

- [ ] A valid ID card or passport.
- [ ] The **original** deposit document: deposit or current account agreement, savings book, account card, or another document under which you disposed of the deposit before 30.10.2026. You show it to the clerk, in Serbian “na uvid” ([what qualifies](#deposit-document)).
- [ ] Want the euros transferred to your account at another bank in Serbia rather than in cash: [that bank's details](#transfer).
- [ ] Had ALTA cheques: the payout is reduced by uncashed cheques; unused cheques go to ALTA banka in bankruptcy, not to Poštanska ([more](#cheques)).

**Non-resident (foreigner):**

- [ ] A passport (or another photo ID) instead of an ID card; everything else as for an individual above.

**Collecting for someone else under a power of attorney:**

- [ ] A **new** authorisation certified by a notary (јавни бележник) dated no earlier than 30.10.2026. Old authorisations for an ALTA account are not accepted ([what this means](#note-poa)).
- [ ] Authorisation made abroad: translated into Serbian and apostilled.
- [ ] The original deposit document of the person you are collecting for.
- [ ] Your own ID card: its number is written into the authorisation on [AOD's form](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/fizicka_lica.doc).
- [ ] Under AOD's form the authorisation is issued in 3 copies: for the depositor, ALTA banka in bankruptcy and Poštanska ([details](#poa-copies)).

**Heir:**

- [ ] The inheritance decision (решење о наслеђивању) and your ID. One heir and a decision covering the whole amount: the heir is paid, and a copy of the decision checked against the original is attached to the request. Several heirs or a decision covering only some deposits: you are referred to the complaint (reklamacija) procedure ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)).

**Guardian; parent of a minor:**

- [ ] Guardian: the guardianship decision (решење о старатељству); parent: the child's birth certificate (извод из матичне књиге рођених). Plus your ID.

Whether an heir, guardian or parent also needs the deposit agreement, AOD has not said: its list puts these documents next to the agreement.

**Sole trader (preduzetnik) and company (micro, small or medium-sized legal entity):**

- [ ] The **original** deposit document (account agreement, account card, etc.).
- [ ] The [OP form](#op-form): original or certified copy.
- [ ] The ID card of whoever comes: the representative entered in the register (the sole trader, the director) or the authorised person; exceptionally a passport or another photo ID.
- [ ] If someone other than the registered representative comes: an [authorisation on letterhead](#memorandum), signed and stamped; under [AOD's form](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/pravna_lica.doc) a register extract no older than 3 months is attached.
- [ ] Under AOD's form the authorisation is issued in 3 copies: for the depositor, ALTA banka in bankruptcy and Poštanska ([details](#poa-copies)).
- [ ] [Account details](#transfer) at a bank in Serbia: sole traders and companies are paid only by transfer.

### Before you sign

Under AOD's procedure, which AOD's notice of 06.11.2026 also follows ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf); verbatim [under the checklist](#before-signing-quotes)):

- **Overview of deposits: two copies, one is yours.** The clerk prints an overview of all your deposits as of 03.11.2026 and the amount to be paid. By signing it you confirm that you have seen it.
- **Signing the request means agreeing with the amount.** The payout request is drawn up if you agree with the amount in the overview. Your copy of the request and the payment document are handed to you at once.
- **Disagree: file a complaint (reklamacija).** The clerk will offer to pay the undisputed amount right away and refer the difference to a complaint. The clerk gives you the form; two copies, yours gets a receipt stamp. Attach copies of your documents.
- **Answer within 15 working days** from the day the complaint reaches ALTA banka in bankruptcy. If the bank asks for documents, the period runs from the day you provide them. Still disagree: complain directly to AOD; its written answer is final.

## What each item means

<span id="note-document"></span>

**Original deposit document.** AOD lists: a cash deposit agreement, a savings deposit agreement, a bank current account agreement, a savings book, a card of a dinar or foreign-currency current account, or another valid document on the basis of which you disposed of the deposit before the licence was revoked on 30.10.2026. Show the original, not a copy. Under AOD's procedure this is an original issued (signed and certified) by the bank before the licence was revoked; without it the request counts as not filed ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)). No contract? See [«If you have no contract»](#no-contract).

> «Приликом подношења захтева за исплату, потребно је да депонент пружи на увид: важећу идентификациону исправу и оригиналну документацију којом се доказује основаност потраживања […]» — [AOD, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf).
>
> «Исправа из тачке 2. став 1. овог одељка се подноси на увид у оргиналном примерку […]» (sic, «оргиналном», in AOD's text) — [AOD procedure, section VIII, p. 19](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf).

“Na uvid” means shown for inspection. Whether the paying bank keeps the original, these documents do not say.

<span id="note-poa"></span>

**Individual's power of attorney.** Another adult can come for you: AOD allows you to authorise them to file the request and collect the money. A notary (javni beležnik) certifies it, and the certification date must be **no earlier than 30.10.2026**, the day the licence was revoked. Older authorisations over an ALTA account, issued before the licence was revoked, cannot be used to collect the insured amount at Banka Poštanska štedionica. The template is [fizicka_lica.doc](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/fizicka_lica.doc) on AOD's site; the rule verbatim is under [«Power of attorney»](#power-of-attorney).

<span id="memorandum"></span>

**Authorisation on letterhead (ovlašćenje na memorandumu).** A power of attorney printed on the company's or sole trader's letterhead. It names the specific person allowed to collect the insured amount and gives the right to dispose of funds up to the insured amount. It is signed by the person entered in the register as legal representative (the company director, the sole trader) and stamped. It is needed **only if someone other than that representative comes to the branch**. The text is on AOD's website and on Poštanska's website and counters; AOD's form for legal entities is [pravna_lica.doc](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/pravna_lica.doc), also below under [«Power of attorney»](#power-of-attorney).

<span id="poa-copies"></span>

**Authorisation in 3 copies.** Both of AOD's authorisation forms, for individuals and for companies and sole traders, say the authorisation is issued in three copies, one each for the depositor, ALTA banka in bankruptcy and the paying bank (Banka Poštanska štedionica). Whether all three copies must be brought to the branch, AOD has not specified.

> «Овлашћење се издаје у 3 примерка, од којих по 1 (један) припада: депоненту, банци у стечају/ ликвидацији и банци исплатиоцу.» — AOD authorisation forms ([individuals](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/fizicka_lica.doc), [legal entities](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/pravna_lica.doc)).

<span id="op-form"></span>

**OP form («Оверени потписи лица овлашћених за заступање»).** Literally «certified signatures of persons authorised to represent»: a document with the specimen signatures of those who, per the register, may act for the company or sole trader, certified by the competent authority. Show the original or a certified copy. It must contain the registered representative's details and, if an authorised person comes, theirs too. AOD's notice does not say which authority certifies it; if you do not have one, ask Poštanska or AOD (contacts [below](#contacts)).

**ID card: whose.** For a company or sole trader, the person who comes: the registered legal representative or the authorised person. Exceptionally a passport or another photo ID is accepted. An individual shows a valid identity document; a non-resident foreigner, a passport.

<span id="exchange-rate"></span>

**Exchange rate of 03.11.2026.** Dinar deposits are paid in dinars. All foreign-currency deposits (euros, dollars, francs and others) are paid **in euros**: a deposit in another currency is converted to euros at the middle rate of the euro against that currency valid on 03.11.2026, the day bankruptcy was opened. For example, a dollar deposit is converted at the euro–dollar middle rate of 03.11.2026. The €50,000 limit applies to all your deposits together.

<span id="transfer"></span>

**Transferring euros to another bank.** If you want the insured amount in euros sent to your foreign-currency account at **another bank in Serbia**, bring that bank's instructions for a foreign-currency transfer. At minimum: the name of the bank where you hold the foreign-currency account; your first and last name; the account number at that bank, i.e. the IBAN, and the bank's SWIFT address. AOD's Q&A on ALTA (item d) gives a different list: the other bank's name, the correspondent bank abroad, the IBAN or account number ([AOD, Q&A on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)). Sole traders and companies are paid only by transfer to an account.

<span id="cheques"></span>

**ALTA cheques.** If ALTA issued you cheques (a chequebook), the insured amount is **reduced by the potential liability for cheques not yet cashed** («умањен за износ потенцијалне обавезе по основу нереализованих чекова»): the bank may still have to pay on them, so that part is held back. What to do: hand in the unused cheques you still hold (settle them, «razdužiti se») at ALTA banka in bankruptcy. The settlement confirmation issued by the bank in bankruptcy is the basis for paying the withheld part of the insured amount, up to the value of the cheques handed in. The notice does not say where or how to hand them in; ask AOD. If you cannot return uncashed cheques because they were lost, stolen or destroyed, the paying-bank employee refers you to the complaint procedure, based on a statement you sign and a confirmation issued by the bank in bankruptcy ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)).

> «Депонентима корисницима чекова издатих од стране АЛТА банке АД Београд биће исплаћен износ осигураних депозита умањен за износ потенцијалне обавезе по основу нереализованих чекова. Депоненти ће се упутити да се за неискоришћене чекове које имају у свом поседу раздуже код АЛТА банке АД Београд у стечају.» — [AOD, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf).

<span id="contacts"></span>

**Contacts for questions and complaints about the amount** ([AOD, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)):

- AOD (Deposit Insurance Agency): Masarikova 5, Belgrade; tel. 011/7358 661; depozit@aod.rs.
- Banka Poštanska štedionica (paying bank): Kraljice Marije 3, Belgrade, retail banking sector; tel. 011/2020 292; aodisplata2026@posted.co.rs.

## How much and in which currency

- Up to €50,000 per depositor: all your accounts and deposits at ALTA are added up, whatever their number, type and currency ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)).
- Dinar deposits are paid in dinars, foreign-currency deposits in euros, at the National Bank of Serbia middle rate of 03.11.2026, the day the bankruptcy was opened.
- If you have both dinars and foreign currency, the insured amount is split in proportion to the currency structure of all your deposits ([AOD, questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori)).
- A person's private accounts and their sole-trader accounts count as two separate depositors, each with its own €50,000 limit ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)).

> «Средства са динарских рачуна исплаћују се у динарима, а са девизних у еврима, прерачунато по средњем курсу Народне банке Србије на дан доношења решења о отварању стечајног поступка (3. новембар 2026. године).» — [AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka).

> «Ако депонент има депозите и у динарима и у девизама, Агенција, преко банке исплатиоца, исплаћује осигурани износ пропорционално према валутној структури укупних депозита депонента у банци у динарима и у девизама.» — [AOD, questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori).

## Who is entitled to the payout and who is not

**Entitled** (per AOD's answers):

- individuals, **both residents and non-residents**;
- sole traders (preduzetnici);
- micro, small and medium-sized legal entities registered in Serbia;
- also, on AOD's list: house councils (of residents), registered farm households, religious associations and citizens' associations, lawyers and law offices, trade unions, sports clubs, companies that are themselves in bankruptcy or liquidation, and any other legal entity that under the Accounting Law falls into the micro, small or medium category.

> «Системом осигурања депозита заштићена су физичка лица (резиденти и нерезиденти), предузетници, као и микро, мала и средња правна лица регистрована у Србији.»
>
> «Осигураним категоријама депонената сматрају се и кућни савети, регистрована пољопривредна домаћинства, верска удружења и удружења грађана, адвокати и адвокатске канцеларије, синдикалне организације, спортски клубови, привредни субјекти у стечају и ликвидацији као и сва друга правна лица која би по критеријумима разврставања правних лица према одредбама Закона о рачуноводству припадала категорији микро, малих, односно средњих правних лица.»
>
> — [AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka).

**Not entitled** (deposits excluded from the insurance):

- of legal entities and individuals connected with the bank;
- payable to a code word or to bearer;
- arising from money laundering or terrorist financing;
- of large legal entities (under the Accounting Law);
- of state bodies and organisations, bodies of the autonomous province or of local self-government;
- of investors whose funds are protected under the Capital Market Law (see [«If you had investments»](#investments));
- a deposit that serves as contractual collateral, if the bank's claim against the depositor secured by that deposit is greater than or equal to the deposit;
- of educational, health, social and other legal entities financed from the budget;
- of bankruptcy and liquidation estates.

> «Из система осигурања депозита искључени су депозити: правних и физичких лица повезаних с банком; који гласе на шифру или доносиоца; настали као последица прања новца или финансирања тероризма; великих правних лица у смислу Закона о рачуноводству; државних органа и организација, органа аутономне покрајине или органа јединице локалне самоуправе; инвеститора, чија су средства заштићена у складу са Законом о тржишту капитала; који представљају уговорено средство обезбеђења, ако је износ потраживања банке према депоненту које је обезбеђено тим депозитом веће или једнако износу тог депозита; образовних, здравствених, социјалних и других правних лица који се финансирају буџетски; стечајних и ликвидационих маса.» — [AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka).

Depositors must themselves file anything above €50,000 as a claim against the bank in bankruptcy ([AOD](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka); more: [«Claim above €50,000»](/en/trebovanie/)).

> «Износ изнад осигураних 50.000 евра може се наплатити у стечајном поступку над банком и депоненти га сами пријављују као потраживање од банке у стечају.» — [AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka).

## Individuals: steps

1. **Go to a Poštanska branch designated for ALTA depositors** from 13.11.2026, during working hours; the deadline is 03.11.2029 ([AOD, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)).
2. **Bring a photo ID and the original deposit document.** ID card or passport; non-residents a passport. The deposit document must be the original; see the list under [«Deposit document»](#deposit-document).
3. **Calculation.** A paying-bank employee prints a calculation of all your deposits: the amount to be paid and its currency structure. You sign to confirm you have seen it ([AOD, questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori); [AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)).
4. **Signature.** If you agree with the amount, the employee prints the payout request and you sign it. You can take cash or ask for a transfer to an account ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)).
5. **Complaint (reklamacija).** If you disagree with the amount or the currency structure, or think the calculation does not match the facts, you file a complaint ([AOD, questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori)). If you believe you are owed more, the paying-bank employee offers you to request the undisputed amount and refers you to the complaint procedure for the difference ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)).

> «Уколико није сагласан с висином или валутном структуром утврђеног права на исплату осигураног износа или ако сматра да то право није сагласно чињеничном стању, депонент подноси рекламацију.» — [AOD, questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori).

If you cannot come yourself, see [«Power of attorney»](#power-of-attorney).

## Sole traders and companies: steps

Sole traders and micro, small and medium-sized legal entities receive the insured amount **only by transfer to an account**; not in cash. The steps are the same: calculation, signature, and a complaint if you disagree.

> «Правним лицима се осигурани износ депозита може исплатити искључиво преносом на рачун.» — [AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka).

> «Исплата осигураног износа депоненту предузетнику, микро, малом и средњем правном лицу врши се безготовински - преносом на рачун који одреди депонент» — [AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf). For sole traders AOD wrote the same to a depositor on 02.11.2026: a transfer to an account at a bank in Serbia ([AOD letters](/en/vyplata/#aod-answers)).

What to show at the branch, per AOD's list ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)):

1. The original deposit document (see [below](#deposit-document)).
2. If the person coming is not the representative entered in the register: an authorisation on the company's letterhead, naming the authorised person, signed by the registered representative and stamped. AOD's form is under [«Power of attorney»](#power-of-attorney).
3. The OP form («Оверени потписи лица овлашћених за заступање») with certified signatures, original or certified copy. It must contain the registered representative's details and, if an authorised person comes, theirs too.
4. The original ID card of the representative or the authorised person; exceptionally a passport or another photo ID.
5. If the euro amount is to be transferred to another bank in Serbia: that bank's foreign-currency transfer instructions with the name of the bank holding the foreign-currency account, the depositor's name, the account number, i.e. the IBAN, and the bank's SWIFT address ([AOD, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)).

## AOD's written answers on the payout up to €50,000

An ALTA depositor who holds money both as an individual and as a sole trader (preduzetnik) asked AOD in writing on 31.10.2026 how the insurance would be counted and paid. AOD replied on 02.11.2026 from depozit@aod.rs. Below is what follows from the answers, and screenshots of the letters: the depositor's question and AOD's highlighted answer. Personal data is blacked out.

1. **The €50,000 limit applies separately to the individual and to the same person as a sole trader.** Personal accounts and sole-trader accounts are not added up: each has its own limit. AOD's answer, citing [art. 2 of the Law on Deposit Insurance](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg): «limit osiguranja depozita od 50.000 evra primenjuje se zasebno na fizičko lice i na isto to lice registrovano kao preduzetnik. Isplata fizičkih lica vrši se u gotovini ili prenosom na drugi račun deponenta a pravnih lica prenosom na drugi račun.» In law a sole trader is a natural person doing business ([Law on Companies, art. 83](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_privrednim_drustvima.html)), which is why AOD's answer matters.
2. **How the money is paid.** Individuals: in cash or by transfer to another account. Sole traders and micro, small and medium-sized companies: by transfer only, to an account the depositor names ([AOD procedure, section VIII item 31](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)): «Исплата осигураног износа депоненту предузетнику, микро, малом и средњем правном лицу врши се безготовински - преносом на рачун који одреди депонент».
3. **Transfers only to an account at a bank in Serbia.** A foreign individual cannot have the insured amount sent abroad: AOD answered «prenos na račun u banci u Srbiji odnosno isplata u gotovini». For a sole trader: «prenos na račun u banci u Srbiji».
4. **Personal or sole-trader account?** AOD has not yet said which account in Serbia a sole trader's money goes to, the sole-trader account or a personal one. The question has been asked; we will add the answer here.

## What you can do with euros at Poštanska

Depositors in chats write that from a foreign-currency account at Poštanska you cannot withdraw euros in cash, transfer them abroad or convert them into dinars — only pay by card. We checked this against the bank's own documents. Note: per AOD's notice of 06.11.2026 the insured amount is paid at Poštanska branches, and on your instructions the euros can be transferred to a foreign-currency account at another bank in Serbia ([AOD, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf); see [«Transferring euros to another bank»](#transfer)). Below are Poštanska's general rules for an individual's foreign-currency payment account (devizni platni račun), as of 06.11.2026.

- **You do not have to keep the insured amount at Poštanska.** An individual can take it in cash or ask for a transfer to an account ([AOD, Q&A on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)); a transfer only to an account at a bank in Serbia ([AOD letters](#aod-answers)).
- **Cash euros: allowed by the bank's documents.** Account page: funds can be used by «подизањем ефективе» (withdrawing foreign cash) at the bank's counters and ATMs, with no withdrawal fee ([Poštanska, FX account](https://www.posted.co.rs/stanovnistvo/platni-racuni/devizni-racun.html)). Tariff, item 2.4.1: «Уплата и исплата у ефективи и динарима — Без накнаде» ([Poštanska tariff](https://www.posted.co.rs/korisno/doc/Tarifa/Tarifa-naknada-za-usluge-u-radu-sa-fizickim-licima.pdf)). Whether a given branch has the amount in cash and whether it must be ordered in advance, the bank does not say in writing — ask at the branch.
- **Transfer abroad (SWIFT) and SEPA: per the tariff, at the counter, not in the app.** Tariff, item 2.6.1.1.2: a transfer in euros to an account abroad at the counter costs 0.5% (min. 300, max. 20,000 RSD) plus the «OUR» charge; item 2.6.1.1.1.7: «SEPA плаћање SCT / НБС клиринг — RSD 500,00»; in the tariff this item belongs to section 2.6.1.1.1 «На платни рачун у Републици Србији» (to a payment account in Serbia), while transfers abroad are section 2.6.1.1.2. For e-banking the tariff (item 2.6.1.2) lists only transfers between your own FX accounts at the bank ([Poštanska tariff](https://www.posted.co.rs/korisno/doc/Tarifa/Tarifa-naknada-za-usluge-u-radu-sa-fizickim-licima.pdf)). For a resident the bank makes a transfer abroad on a stated basis: a personal transfer (gift, help to a family member, inheritance, annuity, emigrant's funds) or a payment against an invoice ([Poštanska, FX account](https://www.posted.co.rs/stanovnistvo/platni-racuni/devizni-racun.html); [Law on Foreign Exchange Operations, art. 2 item 25 and art. 31](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_deviznom_poslovanju.html)).
- **Transfer to your own FX account at another bank in Serbia:** 0.3% (min. 2,000, max. 20,000 RSD) ([Poštanska fee document](https://www.posted.co.rs/korisno/doc/PUN/PUN_Devizni_tekuci_racuni.pdf); [tariff, item 2.6.1.1.1.5](https://www.posted.co.rs/korisno/doc/Tarifa/Tarifa-naknada-za-usluge-u-radu-sa-fizickim-licima.pdf)).
- **Conversion into dinars: per the tariff, at the counter, free.** Tariff, item 2.6.1.1.1.2: «Пренос средстава на динарски платни рачун у банци (интерни пренос) — Без накнаде» ([Poštanska tariff](https://www.posted.co.rs/korisno/doc/Tarifa/Tarifa-naknada-za-usluge-u-radu-sa-fizickim-licima.pdf)). The tariff lists no such operation in the app. The rate at which the bank buys euros is not stated in the tariff.
- **Card: yes.** A Visa card is issued for the FX account: payments in Serbia and abroad, cash at ATMs ([Poštanska, FX account](https://www.posted.co.rs/stanovnistvo/platni-racuni/devizni-racun.html)); cash withdrawal by card at Poštanska counters and ATMs is free ([fee document, item 3.2](https://www.posted.co.rs/korisno/doc/PUN/PUN_Devizni_tekuci_racuni.pdf)). The conversion rate for card payments is not given in the bank's documents.

**Resident or non-resident.** A foreigner with a residence permit or work visa who has lived in Serbia **for more than a year** is a resident under the law; less than a year — a non-resident ([Law on Foreign Exchange Operations, art. 2](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_deviznom_poslovanju.html)). From a non-resident's FX savings account a transfer abroad «vrši se slobodno» (art. 29). Poštanska opens a resident FX account for a foreigner who has lived in Serbia for over a year, with a certificate from the Ministry of the Interior ([Poštanska, FX account](https://www.posted.co.rs/stanovnistvo/platni-racuni/devizni-racun.html)).

> «Располагање средствима са платног девизног рачуна врши се: подизањем ефективе или динарске противвредности, на шалтерима и банкоматима Банке, као и налозима за плаћање и пренос. Провизија за исплату се не наплаћује.» — [Poštanska, FX account](https://www.posted.co.rs/stanovnistvo/platni-racuni/devizni-racun.html).

> «5) fizičko lice - strani državljanin koji na osnovu dozvole za boravak, odnosno radne vize boravi u Republici duže od godinu dana, osim diplomatsko-konzularnih predstavnika stranih zemalja i članova njihovih porodica» — [Law on Foreign Exchange Operations, art. 2](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_deviznom_poslovanju.html).

## Deposit document: the original is required

Besides a photo ID, the depositor shows the **original** of a document proving the deposit. AOD lists ([questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)):

> «уговор о новчаном депозиту, уговор о штедном улогу, уговор о банкарском текућем рачуну, штедна књижица, картица текућег динарског или девизног рачуна, решење о наслеђивању, решење о старатељству, извод из матичне књиге рођених (којим се доказује право законског заступника да располаже средствима - за родитеље малолетних лица), или други валидан документ на основу кога је депонент располагао средствима депозита у периоду пре доношења решења НБС о одузимању дозволе за рад банци (30. октобра 2026. године).»

In English: a cash deposit contract, a savings deposit contract, a current account contract, a savings book, «картица текућег динарског или девизног рачуна», an inheritance decision, a guardianship decision, a birth register extract (for parents of minors) or another valid document under which you used the deposit before the licence was revoked on 30.10.2026. We keep the item «картица рачуна» as AOD wrote it: AOD has not explained what exactly the paying bank will accept under that name.

### If you have no contract

- A contract is not the only option: any document from the list above works, including another valid document under which you used the deposit before 30.10.2026.
- It must be shown **in the original**. Under AOD's procedure, if there is no original you need a certificate from the bank in bankruptcy on your right to use the deposit, or a copy of the contract certified by an authorised person of the bank in bankruptcy; without the original or such a certified document the request counts as not filed ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)). AOD has not yet said how to request such a certificate from ALTA specifically.

## Power of attorney

If you cannot come yourself, someone else can collect the insured amount under a power of attorney. According to AOD's answers for ALTA, the power of attorney is made **at a notary** (javni beležnik); if you are abroad, it must be translated into Serbian and certified with an apostille.

> «Уколико нисте у могућности да сами преузмете новац, потребно је да код јавног бележника израдите овлашћење за друго лице. Уколико се налазите у иностранству, овлашћење мора бити преведено на српски и оверено апостилом.» — [AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka).

On AOD's forms page the rule for individuals reads (in Serbian, verbatim):

> «Депонент физичко лице може после наступања осигураног случаја дати овлашћење другом пунолетном физичком лицу да у његово име и за његов рачун располаже средствима осигураног износа депозита, подноси захтев за исплату и подиже средства осигураног износа депозита, с тим што ово овлашћење мора бити оверено од стране јавног бележника, под датумом не старијим од дана одузимања дозволе за рад банци. Овлашћења којима су депоненти физичка лица овластили трећа лица за располагање средствима на рачунима, а која су издата пре одузимања дозволе за рад банци, не могу се користити приликом подношења захтева за исплату осигураног износа у Банци Поштанска штедионица.» — [AOD forms](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka).

From abroad, add a Serbian translation and an apostille (AOD's answers above).

## If you had investments with ALTA (up to €20,000)

**This does not concern ordinary depositors.** Deposits, current and savings accounts are paid by Poštanska under the rules above. This section is only for small investors who were ALTA's investment-services clients: a brokerage account, securities, money on an investment account. They are covered by a different fund, the Investor Protection Fund, also run by AOD. On 06.11.2026 AOD published a call to these clients ([AOD call, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/FZI_20261106.pdf)):

- **How much:** up to €20,000 per investor, paid **in dinars** at the National Bank's official middle rate of 03.11.2026. All claims against the bank are counted together, whatever the number of accounts, currencies and contracts.
- **What is covered:** money deposited or arising from investment services, and financial instruments (securities) the bank held and managed for the client.
- **How to apply:** fill in [AOD's form «Захтев за исплату заштићеног потраживања»](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/pravnareg/40_pravnareg.pdf) (also available at ALTA banka in bankruptcy, Bulevar Zorana Đinđića 121, Belgrade) and send it by post or hand it in at AOD's registry office (pisarnica): Masarikova 5, 11000 Belgrade. Not at Poštanska.
