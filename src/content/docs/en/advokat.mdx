## Alone or with a lawyer

<div class="adv-split not-content">
<div class="adv-card">
<p class="adv-card-h">On your own</p>
<ul>
<li>You write the claim in free form: there is no ready-made form ([AOD](https://www.aod.rs/clan/stecajlikv/pitanja_odgovori)). What to write: [What to prepare](/en/trebovanie/#what-to-prepare).</li>
<li>You calculate the amount and interest and gather the agreements and statements.</li>
<li>You file with the Commercial Court in Belgrade within 90 days and follow the list of claims yourself.</li>
<li>You can also be represented by a blood relative in the direct line, a brother, a sister or a spouse ([art. 85 LCP](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/72/2/reg)).</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="adv-card adv-card-lawyer">
<p class="adv-card-h">A lawyer</p>
<ul>
<li>Drafts and files the claim with every item of art. 111 of the Bankruptcy Law.</li>
<li>Calculates the amount and checks the documents.</li>
<li>Follows the list of recognised claims; if the claim is disputed, files a lawsuit within 15 days.</li>
<li>Charges under the Bar tariff and must give you a statement ([art. 23](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/31/1/reg)).</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<details class="adv-more">
<summary>In detail: what a lawyer does at the claim stage, and under which articles</summary>

A lawyer drafts applications and other court documents and represents the client ([Law on the Legal Profession, art. 3](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/31/1/reg)). If the power of attorney does not limit the powers, the lawyer may take every procedural step on the client's behalf ([Law on Civil Procedure, art. 89](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/72/2/reg); it applies in bankruptcy under [art. 7 of the Bankruptcy Law](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). For a claim in the ALTA bankruptcy this means:

1. **Draft and file the claim** (prijava potraživanja) with the Commercial Court in Belgrade, with every item the law requires: the creditor's name and address, personal number (JMBG), account number, the legal basis of the claim, the amount — principal and interest calculation shown separately — and what exactly the creditor asks the court to recognise. A foreign-currency claim is filed in that currency ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 111 para. 1–2](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).
2. **Calculate the amount and check the documents**: account and deposit agreements, statements, contractual interest.
3. **File on time**: 90 days from the day the bankruptcy notice is published in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik) (court decision 10 St 275/2026, [item IV](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/docs/dokument_03112026_104425437.pdf); [art. 7 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). How to watch for the publication: see the [Claim page](/en/trebovanie/#glasnik).
4. **Follow the list of recognised claims.** After the deadline AOD, as the bankruptcy administrator, examines every claim and draws up a list of recognised and disputed claims ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 113 para. 2](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg); [art. 17 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). The court posts the list on its notice board (art. 113 para. 4). A creditor whose claim is disputed is notified by AOD in person at least 15 days before the claims examination hearing; at the creditor's request AOD must review the claim again together with the creditor and any new evidence (art. 113 para. 5).
5. **If the claim is disputed, file a lawsuit within 15 days.** The court refers the creditor to civil proceedings; the lawsuit is filed within 15 days of receiving the court's decision on the list of claims. A creditor who does not sue in time loses creditor status for the disputed part (art. 116, [art. 117 para. 1–2](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). The bankruptcy judge must be told about the lawsuit within 15 days (art. 117 para. 4).

</details>

<details class="adv-more">
<summary>Choosing a lawyer: who can represent you, confidentiality, fees</summary>

- A natural person can be represented in proceedings by a lawyer, or by a blood relative in the direct line, a brother, a sister or a spouse ([art. 85 of the Law on Civil Procedure](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/72/2/reg)).
- A lawyer decides freely whether to take a case ([Law on the Legal Profession, art. 18](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/31/1/reg)) and must keep confidential everything the client has entrusted to them (art. 20).
- A lawyer's fee and expenses are calculated under the tariff of the Bar Association of Serbia: the amount depends on the type of proceedings, the step taken and the value at stake. The lawyer must give the client a statement of the fee and expenses (art. 23).

</details>

<ul class="adv-facts">
<li>**Power of attorney** (punomoćje) — written, from each client; the lawyer attaches it to the first document filed ([art. 90 and 91 LCP](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/72/2/reg)).</li>
<li>**Claim** — own name, JMBG, account and amount ([art. 111](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). There is no "collective" claim — [why](/en/voprosy/#class-action-and-a-shared-lawyer).</li>
<li>**The group gets no special rights** — [what teaming up gives](/en/voprosy/#class-action-and-a-shared-lawyer).</li>
</ul>

<details class="adv-more">
<summary>If it comes to a lawsuit: co-plaintiffs</summary>

Several creditors may sue together as co-plaintiffs when their claims are of the same kind and rest on essentially the same facts and legal grounds ([art. 205 para. 1 of the Law on Civil Procedure](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/72/2/reg)). Each co-plaintiff remains an independent party: their actions or omissions neither help nor harm the others (art. 209).

</details>

## How to check a lawyer

<ol class="adv-check not-content">
<li>
<p class="adv-check-h">Listed in the bar directory</p>
<p>A lawyer is a person entered in the directory who has taken the oath ([art. 4](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/31/1/reg)). Search in Latin letters with diacritics: "Đorđević".</p>
<p class="adv-btns">[Bar of Serbia](https://imenik.aks.org.rs/) [Vojvodina](https://akv.org.rs/akv-imenik/) [Belgrade](https://akbgd.org.rs/imenik/)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="adv-check-h">Contact details match</p>
<p>City, office address and phone — as in the directory. Call the number from there. A "jurist" or an agency is not a lawyer: [how to tell](/en/bezopasnost/#how-to-check-a-lawyer).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p class="adv-check-h">Court decisions name them</p>
<p>Lawyers' names are not hidden in published decisions. Enter the surname and "stečaj".</p>
<p class="adv-btns">[Supreme Court decisions](https://www.vrh.sud.rs/sr-lat/odluke-vrhovnog-suda) [Case law](https://sudskapraksa.sud.rs/sudska-praksa)</p>

<details class="adv-more">
<summary>In detail: where to search the directories</summary>

A lawyer is a person entered in a bar association's directory (register) of lawyers who has taken the lawyer's oath ([Law on the Legal Profession, art. 4 item 2](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/31/1/reg)). Checking is free:

1. **[Directory of the Bar Association of Serbia](https://imenik.aks.org.rs/)** — search by first name, surname or ID card number in the bar associations of Belgrade, Niš, Kragujevac, Čačak, Zaječar, Požarevac and Šabac. Type in Latin letters with diacritics: "Đorđević", not "Djordjevic"; Cyrillic is not accepted. The Bar Association warns that each lawyer updates their own details.
2. **Vojvodina and Novi Sad** — [directory of the Bar Association of Vojvodina](https://akv.org.rs/akv-imenik/) (search by surname, first name and city).
3. **Belgrade** — also the [directory of the Bar Association of Belgrade](https://akbgd.org.rs/imenik/).
4. All associations are listed on the [Bar Association of Serbia page](https://aks.org.rs/o-nama/komore/); the link to akb.org.rs there is outdated, use the Belgrade address above.

Compare the city, office address and phone number, and call the number from the directory. How to tell a lawyer from a "jurist" or an agency: [Stay safe: avoid scams](/en/bezopasnost/#how-to-check-a-lawyer).

</details>

<details class="adv-more">
<summary>In detail: how to search court decisions</summary>

Decisions of the higher courts are published in open databases. Names of parties who are private individuals are hidden, but names of lawyers acting as representatives are not ([Supreme Court rules on pseudonymising decisions](https://www.vrh.sud.rs/sr-lat/pravilnik-o-zameni-i-izostavljanju-pseudonimizacijianonimizaciji-podataka-u-sudskim-odlukama-0)). So you can search the text of decisions for a lawyer's surname.

- **[Supreme Court decisions database](https://www.vrh.sud.rs/sr-lat/odluke-vrhovnog-suda)** — free, no registration: the field "Koja sadrži ključne reči" (keywords) or "Koja sadrži deo teksta" (part of the text). Enter the lawyer's surname and, for example, "stečaj".
- **[Case-law database of the Serbian courts](https://sudskapraksa.sud.rs/sudska-praksa)** — decisions of the Supreme Court, the appellate courts, the Commercial Appellate Court and others; there is a filter by subject matter "Стечај, реорганизација и ликвидација" (bankruptcy, reorganisation and liquidation). Search is available on the site.

What to look for in a decision:

- **Which side they represented**: a creditor, the debtor, the bankruptcy administrator or another party. The opening of the decision says whom the lawyer represents ("čiji je punomoćnik … advokat iz …").
- **The outcome**: what the court decided on the appeal or the revision.
- **Whether it was a bankruptcy (stečaj) case** — any company, not necessarily a bank.

</details>
