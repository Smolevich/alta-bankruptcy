These are stories from depositors themselves: selling a flat, a loan or mortgage, a family or joint account, a sole trader or a company. What the person filed, where, and how it ended.

- **Confirmed by a source** — the case links to a document: a law, a decision, an authority's reply.
- **Personal experience** — a story without a document. It is one case, not a rule.

A moderator checks every case before it is published; we do not publish names or account numbers. This is not legal advice: for your own case, talk to a lawyer.

About opening an account at another bank, see the separate [statistics by bank](/account/stats) (in Russian).
