## How to file

1. **Two identical sets:** each with the claim and copies of evidence (contract, statement, account card).
2. **Hand in or send** both sets together: Privredni sud u Beogradu, Masarikova 2, 11000 Beograd.
3. **In person:** a third copy without evidence; the court stamps and dates it.
4. **Deadline:** 4 February 2027. The insured amount up to €50,000 is not claimed in court.

Per the [AOD instructions of 10.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf), pp. 1–2 ([copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20261110172018/https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)).
