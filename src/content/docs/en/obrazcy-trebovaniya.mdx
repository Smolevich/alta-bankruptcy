<aside class="legal-note not-content"><strong>These samples were written by depositors and have not been checked by the court. Use them at your own risk.</strong> The official template is the AOD form: <a href="https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka">AOD forms for ALTA</a>; you can also fill it in on the <a href="/en/trebovanie/#claim-form">“Claim above €50,000”</a> page.</aside>

This is not an instruction and not the “correct” text. These are two versions that ALTA depositors wrote for themselves and posted in the shared chat in November 2026. We removed all personal data and put placeholders in square brackets. Wording, article references and conclusions in the samples are their authors’ opinion; we have not checked them.

The main template is the AOD form ([AOD forms](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka)). AOD says the form is not mandatory: you may write the claim in your own words as long as it contains all required details ([AOD instructions, p. 3](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)). What the claim must contain follows [art. 111 of the Bankruptcy Law](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg); the list is on the [“Claim above €50,000”](/en/trebovanie/#claim-form) page.

## How they differ

- **Sample 1** is a narrative: what was in the accounts, how much was paid out through Banka Poštanska štedionica, what remains. It relies on the “Pregled podataka o depozitima i nerealizovanim čekovima” sheet issued at the insured payout. Suits those who have already received the insured amount.
- **Sample 2** follows the points of art. 111 of the Bankruptcy Law, for a foreigner without a JMBG (passport and EBS). Interest is a separate line; proof of the insured payout is to be attached “when issued”.

## What to attach and how to file

Only what the AOD instructions for ALTA of 10.11.2026 say ([PDF](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf); [web archive copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20261110172018/https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)):

- **Attachments:** copies of evidence — savings contract, bank statement, account card, unpaid invoices, etc. (p. 2).
- **Two identical sets:** each with the claim and copies of all evidence. Both sets are handed in or sent together (p. 1).
- **In person:** a third copy of the claim, without evidence; the court counter stamps and dates it — this is your proof of filing (p. 1).
- **Where:** Privredni sud u Beogradu, Masarikova 2, 11000 Beograd (p. 1).
- **Deadline:** by 4 February 2027; how it is counted is on the [“Claim above €50,000”](/en/trebovanie/#deadline) page.
- **By post:** sending is allowed (“predaju ili šalju”, p. 1), but the instructions do not say whether by ordinary or registered mail or what to keep.
- **The insured amount up to €50,000 is not claimed in court** (p. 1).
