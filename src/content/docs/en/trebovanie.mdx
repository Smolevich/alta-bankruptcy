AOD pays up to €50,000 itself through the paying bank, Banka Poštanska štedionica; no court is involved. Payouts start on 13.11.2026 (see [How to get the insured amount up to €50,000](/en/vyplata/)). Anything you hold at ALTA above that amount you must **file as a claim** in the bankruptcy. Nobody files it for you, and you don't need a class action or a petition: each creditor files their own claim ([art. 7 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg), [art. 111 of the Law on Bankruptcy](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

## Who files

Anyone whose balance at ALTA is above €50,000. The amount is counted across all of a person's accounts and deposits at the bank together, with interest up to the day bankruptcy is opened ([Law on Deposit Insurance, art. 16 para. 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)). A dinar deposit is counted in dinars, a foreign-currency deposit in euros. To check against the limit, dinars and other currencies are converted into euros at the National Bank's middle rate on the day bankruptcy opens (art. 2 item 7 and art. 17 of the same law). If you hold both dinar and foreign-currency deposits, the insured amount is paid in dinars and euros in proportion to each currency's share (art. 17 para. 2). Your claim is your total minus the insurance payout. AOD will calculate the exact figure.

Deposits of people and companies connected with the bank are not insured and do not count towards the €50,000. If a deposit secured a loan at ALTA, only the deposit minus the debt is insured (arts. 2 and 16 para. 2 of the same law). Large companies and state bodies have no insurance at all.

<ForIp lang="en">
A sole trader is insured in the same way, up to €50,000, and above the limit ranks first on equal terms with individuals. Under the AOD procedure, an individual's deposits are added up by personal ID number (JMBG) and a sole trader's by tax number (PIB). So a personal account and a sole-trader account are counted separately, each with its own €50,000 limit ([AOD procedure, 2015](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)). AOD confirmed this in writing on 02.11.2026, see [AOD's written answers](/en/vyplata/#aod-answers).
</ForIp>

## Where

In writing, to the Commercial Court in Belgrade (Privredni sud u Beogradu): the bankruptcy court is the one where the debtor has its seat ([Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111 para. 1 and art. 16](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). Court address: Masarikova 2, Belgrade. The court opened bankruptcy on 03.11.2026; the decision is posted on the [court's electronic notice board](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/index.php?prf=con&id=56), and case progress by number is on the [courts portal](https://tpson.portal.sud.rs/tpprvs/) (how to search: [home page](/en/)). On 04.11.2026 AOD published a claim template; see [below](#claim-form).

**90 days** from the day the bankruptcy notice appeared in the Official Gazette (06.11.2026). The day of publication itself does not count: day 1 is 7 November. Not from the licence revocation and not from 3 November. The court set this deadline in decision 10 St 275/2026 of 03.11.2026 ([item IV](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/docs/dokument_03112026_104425437.pdf)), and AOD's [forms page](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka) says the same. File without waiting for the insurance payout. For comparison, at Univerzal the court gave 60 days.

## The Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik) and the bankruptcy notice

**Text of the notice: [Official Gazette No. 109, item 172 →](https://slgl.pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/api/prins/viewdoc?uuid=653ae8fc-9490-497e-b42a-abcbf2fbd7fe)** ([web archive copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20261108141656/https://slgl.pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/api/prins/viewdoc?uuid=653ae8fc-9490-497e-b42a-abcbf2fbd7fe)). The text itself shows no issue number or date: [issue No. 109 of 06.11.2026 →](https://www.pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/slglrsNP-overview/24686).

**What it is.** “Službeni glasnik Republike Srbije” (Službeni glasnik RS) is Serbia's official gazette. Its notices section (oglasni deo) carries notices from authorities, courts and companies ([Službeni glasnik](https://www.slglasnik.com/sluzbena-glasila/sluzbeni-glasnik)).

**Where the notice of the opening of bankruptcy appears.** The court must post it on its notice board and electronic notice board, print it in one high-circulation daily newspaper and publish it in the Official Gazette ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 71 para. 3](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). For banks this law applies wherever the Law on Bankruptcy of Banks does not say otherwise ([art. 22](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)).

**What it contains.** Everything in the court decision ([art. 71 para. 4](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)): the court; the bank's name, registration number and address; the administrator (for ALTA, AOD); the call to creditors to file claims and the deadline; the call to the bank's debtors to pay; the date, time and place of the claim review hearing; the day the notice was posted on the court board ([art. 70](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). For ALTA the decision gives no hearing date: it is held no later than 60 days after the six-month claim review period ends (court decision, items VI–VII).

**Why it matters.** The 90 days for a claim run from publication in the Official Gazette, not from the court decision (10 St 275/2026, item IV; [Law on Bankruptcy of Banks, art. 7](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). If you are late, you can still file, but no later than 120 days after publication. To do so you must pay an advance for an extra court hearing. After that the court rejects the claim ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 111 paras. 5 and 8](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

**How it went with Univerzal banka.** The court opened bankruptcy on 03.02.2014, the notice appeared in Official Gazette no. 14 of 07.02.2014 and the filing deadline was 60 days ([Gazette page, web archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20160830214352/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-10-sluzbeni-glasnik-univerzal-banka.pdf)). Three days after the court decision AOD published instructions on its website on how to file a claim ([AOD instructions](https://web.archive.org/web/20160831012046/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-06-uputstvo-za-podnosenje-prijave-potrazivanja.pdf)).

## What to prepare

### For the payout up to €50,000: at the paying bank

This payout comes first. The paying bank needs a photo ID and documents proving the deposit (AOD procedure, [section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)). Step by step, for individuals, sole traders and companies: [How to get the insured amount up to €50,000](/en/vyplata/). Gather in advance:

- A valid identity document ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)); AOD's questions and answers on ALTA say ID card (lična karta) or passport ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)).
- The original deposit document: a cash deposit agreement, a savings deposit agreement, a current account agreement, a savings book, a card of a dinar or foreign-currency current account, or another valid document on the basis of which you disposed of the deposit before 30.10.2026 ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)).
- Heirs: the inheritance decision; guardians: the guardianship decision; parents of minors: the birth register extract. This is from AOD's questions and answers on ALTA; the AOD notice of 06.11.2026 does not list these documents.
- If someone other than the account holder collects the money: a power of attorney ([AOD forms](#authorisation)) made at a notary and certified no earlier than 30.10.2026, and from abroad translated into Serbian and apostilled.

#### Authorisation to collect the insured amount (ovlašćenje)

If you cannot come to a Banka Poštanska štedionica branch yourself, another person can collect the insured amount up to €50,000 under an authorisation. The authorisation forms (for an individual and for a company) are [in the AOD forms block](#claim-form).

The authorised person can collect funds up to the insured amount and sign the documents needed for the payout. Both forms are made in three copies: for the depositor, the bank in bankruptcy and the paying bank. For a company or sole trader, if someone other than the registered representative comes, AOD requires an authorisation on company letterhead naming that person, signed by the representative and stamped, plus the OP form and the authorised person's ID card (a passport as an exception) ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)).

**Certification and documents from abroad.** According to AOD's answers for ALTA, the power of attorney is made at a notary (javni beležnik); if you are abroad, it is translated into Serbian and certified with an apostille ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)). On AOD's forms page, the form for individuals says the power of attorney must be certified by a notary, dated no earlier than 30.10.2026 (the day the licence was revoked); powers of attorney issued before the revocation are not accepted at Poštanska ([AOD forms](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka)). If you have no JMBG, [see below, at the claim template](#claim-form).

### For the claim above €50,000: at the court

**AOD forms.** AOD has published three forms: the claim template for the court, the authorisation for an individual and the authorisation for a company. All are in Serbian Cyrillic; the claim is addressed to the Commercial Court in Belgrade, the authorisations let another person collect the insured amount up to €50,000. What to fill in is listed under each card.

**If you have no JMBG.** None of the three AOD forms says how to fill in the JMBG field if you do not have one (for example, a foreigner without a Serbian personal number); the court and AOD have not announced it separately either.

The template is not mandatory: AOD says the creditor may also write the claim in free form, but it must contain the mandatory items of art. 111 of the Law on Bankruptcy ([AOD, questions and answers on bankruptcy](https://www.aod.rs/clan/stecajlikv/pitanja_odgovori)). AOD repeats these items in its questions and answers on ALTA ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka); [Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111 para. 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)):

- name (for a company, its registered name), address and contact address;
- JMBG (for a company, its registration number);
- account number;
- the legal basis: the account or deposit agreement;
- the amount, with the principal and the interest calculation shown separately;
- for a secured claim, the asset over which you have a separate-satisfaction right (razlučno pravo) and the part of the claim that is not secured;
- what exactly you are asking the court to recognise.

A foreign-currency claim is filed in that currency (para. 2). If the claim is in litigation, the claim states the court and the case number (para. 3). AOD has not published a separate list of attachments for ALTA: the template leaves blank lines for the evidence.

You can file the claim yourself or through a lawyer; what a lawyer can do and how to check one: [A lawyer: why, how to choose and how to check](/en/advokat/).

For Univerzal banka in 2014 AOD also asked for: the subject line “Пријава потраживања на име депозита код … банке у стечају”; how much the paying bank paid and when; what you claim plus contractual interest; the account and bank for payment; name, address, phone and JMBG under your signature; the contracts attached ([AOD instructions, 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20160831012046/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-06-uputstvo-za-podnosenje-prijave-potrazivanja.pdf)). For ALTA the instructions may differ.

## Payment ranks

First, the bank's money covers the costs of the procedure, unpaid wages and the insurance AOD paid out. What is left is paid out by rank ([arts. 19 and 20](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). Within one rank, creditors are paid in proportion to their claims for as long as the money lasts.

- **Rank I**: private individuals, sole traders and micro, small and medium-sized companies, for the amount above €50,000. All on equal terms.
- **Rank II**: taxes and other public revenues falling due in the 3 months before bankruptcy opened (except employees' pension contributions).
- **Rank III**: the National Bank.
- **Rank IV**: other deposits: large companies, state bodies, persons connected with the bank.
- **Rank V**: other creditors.
- **Rank VI**: subordinated debt.
- **Rank VII**: claims of creditors who are also shareholders of the bank (e.g. their loans to it); share capital is returned only from any surplus after all creditors (art. 20 item 7).

How it ended at Univerzal: rank I received about 96.5% of recognised claims after 6 years 7 months to 7 years 4 months, with no interest; rank IV about 17.5% (calculation and sources: [Past cases](/en/kak-bylo/)).
