AOD pays up to €50,000 itself through the paying bank, Banka Poštanska štedionica; no court is involved. The insured amount is paid from 13.11.2026 (see [How to get the insured amount up to €50,000](/en/vyplata/)). Anything you hold at ALTA above that amount you must **file as a claim** in the bankruptcy. Nobody files it for you, and you don't need a class action or a petition: each creditor files their own claim ([art. 7 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg), [art. 111 of the Law on Bankruptcy](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

## Who files

Anyone whose balance at ALTA is above €50,000. The amount is counted across all of a person's accounts and deposits at the bank together, with interest up to the day bankruptcy is opened ([Law on Deposit Insurance, art. 16 para. 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)). A dinar deposit is counted in dinars, a foreign-currency deposit in euros. To check against the limit, dinars and other currencies are converted into euros at the National Bank's middle rate on the day bankruptcy opens (art. 2 item 7 and art. 17 of the same law). If you hold both dinar and foreign-currency deposits, the insured amount is paid in dinars and euros in proportion to each currency's share (art. 17 para. 2). Your claim is your total minus the insurance payout. AOD will calculate the exact figure.

Deposits of people and companies connected with the bank are not insured and do not count towards the €50,000. If a deposit secured a loan at ALTA, only the deposit minus the debt is insured (arts. 2 and 16 para. 2 of the same law). Large companies and state bodies have no insurance at all.

<ForIp lang="en">
A sole trader is insured in the same way, up to €50,000, and above the limit ranks first on equal terms with individuals. Under the AOD procedure, an individual's deposits are added up by personal ID number (JMBG) and a sole trader's by tax number (PIB). So a personal account and a sole-trader account are counted separately, each with its own €50,000 limit ([AOD procedure, 2015](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)). AOD confirmed this in writing on 02.11.2026, see [AOD's written answers](/en/vyplata/#aod-answers).
</ForIp>

## Where

In writing, to the Commercial Court in Belgrade (Privredni sud u Beogradu): the bankruptcy court is the one where the debtor has its seat ([Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111 para. 1 and art. 16](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). Court address: Masarikova 2, Belgrade. The court opened bankruptcy on 03.11.2026; the decision is posted on the [court's electronic notice board](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/index.php?prf=con&id=56), and on 09.11.2026 AOD also posted a scan of it ([court decision, PDF on AOD's site](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/oglas/242_1_oglas.pdf)), and case progress by number is on the [courts portal](https://tpson.portal.sud.rs/tpprvs/) (how to search: [home page](/en/)). On 04.11.2026 AOD published a claim template; see [below](#claim-form).

**How to file: AOD's instructions for ALTA of 10.11.2026** ([PDF](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf); [web archive copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20261110172018/https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)).

- The claim is filed in writing: printed or legibly handwritten (page 1).
- Prepare **two identical sets**. Each holds the claim and copies of all the evidence (contract, statement, invoices and the like). Both sets are handed in to the court together or sent to it (page 1).
- If you file in person, bring a **third copy** of the claim, without the evidence. The clerk at the court counter stamps and dates it. You keep this copy: it proves you filed on time (page 1).
- The insured amount up to €50,000 is not claimed in court: it is collected at Banka Poštanska štedionica from 13.11.2026 (page 1).

> «За Привредни суд је потребно припремити два потпуно иста пакета документације, од којих сваки садржи: пријаву потраживања и копије свих доказа (уговор, извод, рачуни и слично). Та два пакета заједно се предају или шаљу Привредном суду.»
>
> «Потраживања се не пријављују Привредном суду за осигурани износ до 50.000 евра, који се преузима у Банци Поштанска штедионица почев од 13. новембра 2026. године.» — [AOD instructions, page 1](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)

## By when

<ClaimDeadline lang="en" />

**90 days** from the day the bankruptcy notice appeared in the Official Gazette (06.11.2026). Not from the licence revocation and not from 3 November. The court set this deadline in decision 10 St 275/2026 of 03.11.2026 ([item IV](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/docs/dokument_03112026_104425437.pdf)), and AOD's [forms page](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka) and [AOD's instructions of 10.11.2026, page 1](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf) say the same. The deadline runs from publication ([art. 7](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)): the notice appeared in No. 109 of 06.11.2026. File without waiting for the insurance payout. For comparison, at Univerzal the court gave 60 days.

**Why it matters.** The 90 days for a claim run from publication in the Official Gazette, not from the court decision (10 St 275/2026, item IV; [Law on Bankruptcy of Banks, art. 7](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). If you are late, you can still file, but no later than 120 days after publication. To do so you must pay an advance for an extra court hearing. After that the court rejects the claim ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 111 paras. 5 and 8](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

**How it went with Univerzal banka.** The court opened bankruptcy on 03.02.2014, the notice appeared in Official Gazette no. 14 of 07.02.2014 and the filing deadline was 60 days ([Gazette page, web archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20160830214352/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-10-sluzbeni-glasnik-univerzal-banka.pdf)). Three days after the court decision AOD published instructions on its website on how to file a claim ([AOD instructions](https://web.archive.org/web/20160831012046/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-06-uputstvo-za-podnosenje-prijave-potrazivanja.pdf)). For ALTA, AOD published its own instructions on 10.11.2026; [see “Where”](#where).

## What to prepare

### For the payout up to €50,000: at the paying bank

The payout up to €50,000 is separate: [what to bring](/en/vyplata/#what-to-bring).

<details>

An ID and a document proving the claim, with a copy (deposit or account agreement, passbook, account card, inheritance decision). For ALTA, AOD published its own list on 06.11.2026 ([what to bring](/en/vyplata/#what-to-bring)); the document is shown only in the original ([AOD announcement, 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20140308161705/http://www.aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-03-saopstenje.pdf)).

#### Authorisation to collect the insured amount (ovlašćenje)

If you cannot come to a Banka Poštanska štedionica branch yourself, another person can collect the insured amount up to €50,000 under an authorisation. The authorisation forms (for an individual and for a company) are [in the AOD forms block](#claim-form).

The authorised person can collect funds up to the insured amount and sign the documents needed for the payout. Both forms are made in three copies: for the depositor, the bank in bankruptcy and the paying bank. For a company or sole trader, if someone other than the registered representative comes, AOD requires an authorisation on company letterhead naming that person, signed by the representative and stamped, plus the OP form and the authorised person's ID card (a passport as an exception) ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)).

**Certification and documents from abroad.** According to AOD's answers for ALTA, the power of attorney is made at a notary (javni beležnik); if you are abroad, it is translated into Serbian and certified with an apostille ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)). On AOD's forms page, the form for individuals says the power of attorney must be certified by a notary, dated no earlier than 30.10.2026 (the day the licence was revoked); powers of attorney issued before the revocation are not accepted at Poštanska ([AOD forms](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka)). If you have no JMBG, [see below, at the claim template](#claim-form).

### For the claim above €50,000: at the court

**AOD forms.** AOD has published three forms: the claim template for the court, the authorisation for an individual and the authorisation for a company. All are in Serbian Cyrillic; the claim is addressed to the Commercial Court in Belgrade, the authorisations let another person collect the insured amount up to €50,000. What to fill in is listed under each card.

<FormCards fill lang="en" forms={['prijava', 'fizicka', 'pravna']} />

<DocEditor doc={prijava} lang="en" />

**If you have no JMBG.** Neither the three AOD forms nor AOD's instructions of 10.11.2026 say how to fill in the JMBG field if you do not have one (for example, a foreigner without a Serbian personal number); the court and AOD have not announced it separately either.

The template is not mandatory: AOD says the creditor may also write the claim in free form, but it must contain the mandatory items of art. 111 of the Law on Bankruptcy ([AOD, questions and answers on bankruptcy](https://www.aod.rs/clan/stecajlikv/pitanja_odgovori)). AOD's instructions for ALTA of 10.11.2026 say the same: the template is optional, and you may write the claim in your own words, by hand or on a computer, as long as it has all the key information ([AOD instructions, page 3](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)). What the claim must contain, per AOD's instructions for ALTA and art. 111 of the Law on Bankruptcy ([AOD instructions, pages 1–3](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf); [AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka); [Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111 para. 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)):

- **Your details:** first name, surname, home address, phone and email if you have one. For a company: its exact registered name and seat address.
- **Number:** JMBG; for a company, its registration number (MB).
- **Account for payment:** your current or dinar account at another, operating bank. The money is transferred there when payments to creditors start.
- **Legal basis:** why the bank owes you. AOD's examples: «Новац на динарском/девизном штедном рачуну» (money in a dinar or foreign-currency savings account), «Средства на текућем рачуну» (funds in a current account). Copies of the evidence must be attached ([which](#claim-attachments)).
- **Exact amount:** principal and interest (if any) separately, in the currency the bank actually owes you (for example €2,000 or 15,000 dinars). AOD's example: «Главни дуг на дан 3.11.2026. износи 100.000 динара, а камата износи 1.500 динара. Укупно: 101.500 динара.»
- **Security:** if you hold a mortgage or pledge over the bank's assets, state what kind (razlučno pravo). An ordinary depositor or supplier most likely has none, the instructions say: skip this item or write that the debt is «необезбеђен» (unsecured).
- **Request to the court:** for example «Тражим да суд прихвати ову пријаву и призна моје потраживање у укупном износу од [износ] динара/евра» (I ask the court to accept this claim and recognise it in the total amount of …).

**If you cannot calculate the interest** ([AOD instructions, page 2](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)). Write the principal as shown in your documents. The bank has already added the contractual interest on your savings to the balance in your last statement as of 03.11.2026. Without a statement, write the savings amount and add: «Тражим и уговорену камату од [проценат]% према Уговору о штедњи бр. [број уговора], од [датум] до 3.11.2026. године». The administrator calculates it. If you claim default interest because the bank paid late, add: «Тражим и законску затезну камату од дана када је дуг настао па све до 3.11.2026. године, а коју ће обрачунати стечајни управник».

**Special cases** ([AOD instructions, page 3](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)). If you were already suing ALTA before bankruptcy, state the court and case number (also art. 111 para. 3). For guarantors the instructions say: «Ако сте као жирант (јемац) морали да платите дуг Алта банке након 3.11.2026. године, имате право да тражите да вам се тај новац врати кроз стечајни поступак».

A foreign-currency claim is filed in that currency (art. 111 para. 2). AOD's instructions say the same: «Износ дуга уписује се у оригиналној валути» (page 2), so no conversion into dinars is needed.

You can file the claim yourself or through a lawyer; what a lawyer can do and how to check one: [A lawyer: why, how to choose and how to check](/en/advokat/).

### What documents to attach to the claim

**What AOD's instructions for ALTA say.** Copies of the evidence must be attached to the claim. Each of the two sets for the court contains copies of all the evidence ([AOD instructions, pages 1–2](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf); [how to file](#where)).

> «Уз пријаву обавезно приложите копије доказа (уговор о штедњи, извод из банке, картицу рачуна, неплаћене фактуре и сл.).» — [AOD instructions, page 2](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)

That is: savings agreement, bank statement, account card, unpaid invoices and the like.

**What the law says.** Art. 111 of the Law on Bankruptcy lists what the claim itself must contain (list [above](#claim-form)). It has no separate rule on which documents or evidence to attach ([Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

**What AOD's template has.** In AOD's claim template the amount is claimed “на основу чињеница, обрачуна и доказа наведених у наставку” (on the basis of the facts, calculation and evidence set out below). Then comes a section “Докази који се прилажу уз пријаву” (evidence attached to the claim) with blank lines. Which documents to list there: see AOD's instructions above ([AOD forms](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka); template [above](#claim-form)).

**How AOD proves a deposit.** For the insured payout AOD lists the documents that “доказује основаност потраживања” (prove that the claim is well-founded): cash deposit agreement, savings deposit agreement, current account agreement, savings book, current account card, inheritance decision, guardianship decision, birth register extract (for parents of minors), or another valid document under which you disposed of the deposit before 30.10.2026 ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)). AOD gave this list for Poštanska; for the court, see the list in AOD's instructions above.

**Copies to the court, originals kept.** Under AOD's instructions the court gets copies of the evidence ([pages 1–2](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf)); the instructions do not ask for originals to be filed. At Poštanska, by contrast, the deposit document is shown in the original ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf); [what to bring](/en/vyplata/#what-to-bring)).

**Who checks the amount.** AOD, as administrator, establishes whether each filed claim is well-founded and its amount, within six months after the filing period ends ([court decision, item VI](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/docs/dokument_03112026_104425437.pdf)). **Not established:** whether a claim with a wrong amount will be returned for correction or will have to be filed again; neither the court nor AOD has said.

> «Поверилац благовремено пријављује потраживање према стечајном дужнику у укупном износу од […], са стањем на дан отварања стечајног поступка, и то на основу чињеница, обрачуна и доказа наведених у наставку […] Докази који се прилажу уз пријаву: […]» — [AOD claim template](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka).
>
> «Агенција је дужна да утврди основаност и висину пријављених потраживања поверилаца у року од шест месеци од дана истека рока за њихово пријављивање.» — [court decision 10 St 275/2026, item VI](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/docs/dokument_03112026_104425437.pdf).

### If you no longer have the contract

What is officially known as of 10.11.2026:

- **Statement as of 03.11.2026.** According to AOD's instructions, the contractual interest is shown in your last statement as of 03.11.2026. Without a statement, write the savings amount and the sentence on contractual interest; the administrator calculates it ([AOD instructions, page 2](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo.pdf); [more](#claim-form)). What to do if you have no contract either, the instructions do not say.
- **Overview of deposits from Poštanska.** When you collect the insured amount, the clerk prints an overview of all your deposits as of 03.11.2026 in two copies, one for you ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf); [before you sign](/en/vyplata/#before-signing)). Whether the court accepts this overview as evidence, nothing official says.
- **Statements and confirmations you already have:** ALTA emails with statements, PDFs, text messages, paper receipts. New statements cannot be obtained: «Платне услуге и електронски сервиси банке више нису у функцији и сви рачуни клијената су затворени» ([altabanka.rs](https://altabanka.rs/); [what to keep](/en/vypiski/)).
- **Confirmation from the bank in bankruptcy.** For the insured payout, AOD's procedure says that without the original the bank in bankruptcy issues a confirmation of the right to dispose of the deposit or a certified copy of the agreement ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf); [more](/en/vyplata/#no-contract)). Whether a confirmation of the deposit balance will be issued for a court claim, and how to request it, has not been announced. **AOD has not yet answered a question about a confirmation of the deposit balance** (question sent on 03.11.2026).
- **Not established:** whether a claim with a wrong amount will be returned for correction or will have to be filed again ([see above](#claim-attachments)).

## Payment ranks

First, the bank's money covers the costs of the procedure, unpaid wages and the insurance AOD paid out. What is left is paid out by rank ([arts. 19 and 20](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). Within one rank, creditors are paid in proportion to their claims for as long as the money lasts.

- **Rank I**: private individuals, sole traders and micro, small and medium-sized companies, for the amount above €50,000. All on equal terms.
- **Rank II**: taxes and other public revenues falling due in the 3 months before bankruptcy opened (except employees' pension contributions).
- **Rank III**: the National Bank.
- **Rank IV**: other deposits: large companies, state bodies, persons connected with the bank.
- **Rank V**: other creditors.
- **Rank VI**: subordinated debt.
- **Rank VII**: claims of creditors who are also shareholders of the bank (e.g. their loans to it); share capital is returned only from any surplus after all creditors (art. 20 item 7).

How it ended at Univerzal: rank I received about 96.5% of recognised claims after 6 years 7 months to 7 years 4 months, with no interest; rank IV about 17.5% (calculation and sources: [Past cases](/en/kak-bylo/)).
