AOD repays up to €50,000 itself: you only need to apply. Anything you hold at ALTA above that amount you must **file as a claim** in the bankruptcy. Nobody files it for you, and you don't need a class action or a petition: each creditor files their own claim ([art. 7 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg), [art. 111 of the Law on Bankruptcy](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

## Who files

Anyone whose balance at ALTA is above €50,000. The amount is counted across all of a person's accounts and deposits at the bank together, with interest up to the day bankruptcy is opened ([Law on Deposit Insurance, art. 16 para. 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)). A dinar deposit is counted in dinars, a foreign-currency deposit in euros. To check against the limit, dinars and other currencies are converted into euros at the National Bank's middle rate on the day bankruptcy opens (art. 2 item 7 and art. 17 of the same law). If you hold both dinar and foreign-currency deposits, the insured amount is paid in dinars and euros in proportion to each currency's share (art. 17 para. 2). Your claim is your total minus the insurance payout. AOD will calculate the exact figure.

Deposits of people and companies connected with the bank are not insured and do not count towards the €50,000. If a deposit secured a loan at ALTA, only the deposit minus the debt is insured (arts. 2 and 16 para. 2 of the same law). Large companies and state bodies have no insurance at all.

<ForIp lang="en">
A sole trader is insured in the same way, up to €50,000, and above the limit ranks first on equal terms with individuals. Under the AOD procedure, an individual's deposits are added up by personal ID number (JMBG) and a sole trader's by tax number (PIB). So a personal account and a sole-trader account are counted separately, each with its own €50,000 limit ([AOD procedure, 2015](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)). AOD will confirm how this applies to ALTA.
</ForIp>

## Where

In writing, to the Commercial Court in Belgrade (Privredni sud u Beogradu): the bankruptcy court is the one where the debtor has its seat ([Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111 para. 1 and art. 16](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). The case number, address and form will be announced by the court and AOD together with the decision to open bankruptcy.

## By when

The court sets the deadline: **30 to 90 days** from publication of the notice in the Official Gazette, not from the licence revocation ([art. 7](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). File without waiting for the insurance payout. At Univerzal the court gave 60 days.

## What to prepare

The court or AOD will say what to attach. The law already sets out what the claim itself must contain ([Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111 para. 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)): name, address and contact address; JMBG; account number; the legal basis (account or deposit agreement); the amount, with the principal and the interest calculation shown separately; and what exactly you are asking for. A foreign-currency claim is filed in that currency (para. 2).

Separately, to collect the insured amount at the paying bank the AOD procedure ([section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)) requires a photo ID and documents proving the deposit. It helps to gather in advance:

- Passport. If you have a residence permit, take it too.
- Current and foreign-currency account agreements, the deposit agreement.
- Account and deposit numbers, statements showing the balance on the latest available date.
- If someone other than the account holder collects the money: a power of attorney certified by a court or the municipality; heirs need the inheritance decision (AOD procedure, section VIII).

For comparison, Univerzal depositors in 2014 were asked for an ID and a document proving the claim, with a copy (deposit or account agreement, passbook, account card, inheritance decision). This is an example, not the list for ALTA ([AOD announcement, 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20140308161705/http://www.aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-03-saopstenje.pdf)).

## Payment ranks

First, the bank's money covers the costs of the procedure, unpaid wages and the insurance AOD paid out. What is left is paid out by rank ([arts. 19 and 20](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). Within one rank, creditors are paid in proportion to their claims for as long as the money lasts.

- **Rank I**: private individuals, sole traders and micro, small and medium-sized companies, for the amount above €50,000. All on equal terms.
- **Rank II**: taxes and other public revenues falling due in the 3 months before bankruptcy opened (except employees' pension contributions).
- **Rank III**: the National Bank.
- **Rank IV**: other deposits: large companies, state bodies, persons connected with the bank.
- **Rank V**: other creditors.
- **Rank VI**: subordinated debt.
- **Rank VII**: claims of creditors who are also shareholders of the bank (e.g. their loans to it); share capital is returned only from any surplus after all creditors (art. 20 item 7).

How it ended at Univerzal: the court ordered 100% of recognised rank I claims to be paid out, in three parts (50%, 40% and 10%) and with no interest for the years of waiting. About 96.5% has actually reached creditors; the rest is held in AOD's reserve. Large companies (rank IV) received about 17.5%, ranks V and VI nothing ([AOD report, II/2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/kvartalniizvestaj/Univerzal%20banka_II%20kvartal%202026.pdf)).
