## In short

- Insurance up to the limit was paid quickly in all past cases where this is known: 1 to 7 days after the licence was revoked.
- From 2012 to 2018, six banks lost their licences. In five cases deposits were transferred to another bank (to Nova Agrobanka, later to Poštanska štedionica) and the deposits were moved in full. Uninsured amounts are directly confirmed in three of the five cases (Nova Agrobanka and Razvojna banka Vojvodine by official and government statements, Privredna banka Beograd by a Ministry of Finance statement); for Jugbanka, amounts above the limit were not reported separately. The transfers were not free: the gap between the liabilities taken over and the assets was covered by the AOD deposit insurance fund or by the state with bonds. A transfer was possible only for state-controlled banks, under [Law 102/2012](https://www.nbs.rs/export/sites/NBS_site/documents/propisi/zakoni/kpb_banke_preuzimanje.pdf) (in force until the end of 2014). The National Bank has not announced such a transfer for ALTA: as with Univerzal banka in 2014, AOD will itself pay the insured amount up to the limit, on application.
- At Univerzal, individuals, sole traders, small and medium-sized companies received the first 50% above the limit after 6 years 7 months (09.2020) and the last 10% after 7 years 4 months (06.2021). By court decisions 100% of recognised rank I claims was distributed (50%, 40% and 10%, without interest for the years of waiting); about 96.5% has actually been paid, and the remaining 72.8 million dinars sit in AOD's reserve.
- Large companies (rank IV) at Univerzal had received about 17.5% by 06.2026, and the case is still open.
- We found no lawsuits by depositors against AOD or the state for amounts above the limit.

## Univerzal banka 2014: who received what

What has been announced for ALTA is on the page [Procedure and timeline](/en/procedura/): AOD itself pays the insured amount, as it did for Univerzal in 2014. Univerzal is a guide, but only a guide: court deadlines, the size of the bankruptcy estate and the final percentages at ALTA may differ.

Univerzal: the licence was revoked on 31.01.2014, bankruptcy was opened on 03.02.2014 (case "1 St 19/2014"), the administrator is AOD. Claims recognised: 9,383 million dinars. The figures below are from AOD reports as of Q2 2026.

In bankruptcy creditors are divided into ranks: a lower rank is paid only after the higher rank has been paid in full, and within a rank everyone is paid in proportion to their claim ([art. 54 of the Law on Bankruptcy](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

- **Insured depositors.** Up to €50,000: payout from 04.02.2014 through Poštanska, about €85 million. By the end of 2014, 97 to 98% of the amount due had been paid; the rest collected later (3-year deadline).
- **Rank I** (individuals, sole traders, micro, small and medium-sized companies, amount above the limit; 1,748 creditors). 2,087 million dinars recognised. By court decisions 100% was distributed: 50% (decision of 18.08.2020, paid from 09.2020, after 6 years 7 months), 40% (decision of 07.04.2021) and 10% (decision of 15.06.2021). About 96.5% has actually been paid; the other 72.8 million dinars are held in AOD's reserve "for payment of remaining obligations", and payment needs the creditor's account details.
- **Ranks II and III** (taxes, National Bank): 100%.
- **Rank IV** (under the old law, only deposits of large companies): 6,525 million dinars recognised, about 17.5% paid.
- **Rank V** (other creditors) and **VI** (subordinated debt): nothing.

The Univerzal case continues under the 2015 law (14/2015), but the order of ranks in cases opened under the old law 61/2005 is set by art. 17 of the old law (art. 33 of law 14/2015). Under it, rank IV holds only deposits of large legal entities, except those linked to the bank and bearer or code-word deposits; other deposits go to rank V. At ALTA, ranks follow the new art. 20: rank IV takes all deposits except bank deposits, so Univerzal's percentages do not carry over to ALTA.

Rank I claims in the list of the first distribution (draft of 16.07.2020): 1,746 creditors, the median was 6,230 dinars (about €53), and 1,401 of them had less than 117,000 dinars (about €1,000). By July 2020, before any rank was paid, AOD had recovered all the insurance it paid out from the bank's assets (distribution draft of 16.07.2020).

Money above the limit was paid to creditors who sent AOD their details themselves (a copy of the ID card, account number; heirs, documents proving succession).

## Why the Univerzal case is still open after almost 13 years
On 30.06.2026 the bank's remaining assets (the estate) were valued at 2,444.6 million dinars at liquidation value: cash 252 million, real estate 152 million, shares 409 million, money owed to the bank 313 million, other 1,225 million. Procedure costs are about 35 million dinars a quarter; disputes over contested claims remain. If everything were sold at this liquidation value, rank IV would receive at most about 54% of the recognised amount.

## Other cases

- **2002.** Beogradska, Beobanka, Investbanka, Jugobanka. Payout in 7 days through Poštanska; individuals got their whole deposit back except the old foreign-currency savings. The Beogradska banka and Jugobanka bankruptcies are still open (24 years); Investbanka was closed in 2025 after 23 years. The money for the payout came from the state budget, not from a transfer of deposits ([AOD monograph, pp. 58–59](https://www.aod.rs/downloads/aod_monografija.pdf)).
- **Agrobanka, 25.05.2012.** The National Bank said on the day of the revocation ([notice](http://web.archive.org/web/20120530183826/http://www.nbs.rs:80/internet/cirilica/scripts/showContent.html?id=5797&konverzija=no)) that not only insured but all deposits were moved to the successor bank Nova Agrobanka. Bankruptcy closed by court decision in 2023 ([AOD list](https://www.aod.rs/clan/stecajlikv/postupci_okon)); the remaining assets went to the owners of capital ([AOD report, Q3 2024](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/kvartalniizvestaj/605_kvartalni_izvestaj.pdf)), so creditors were paid practically in full.
- **Nova Agrobanka, 27.10.2012.** According to AOD, part of the liabilities, "including all deposits", moved to Poštanska ([AOD monograph, p. 62](https://www.aod.rs/downloads/aod_monografija.pdf)). The Government said Poštanska was taking over all insured and uninsured deposits ([RTV](https://www.rtv.rs/sr_lat/politika/imovina-i-obaveze-nove-agrobanke-u-postanskoj-stedionici_349826.html)). Access from 29.10 ([National Bank notice](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=6103)). The gap (€281.4 million) was covered by the AOD deposit insurance fund, non-repayable ([AOD report for 2013](https://web.archive.org/web/20161116045606/http://aod.rs/pdf/godisnji_finansijski_izvestaj_za_2013.pdf)). Bankruptcy is still open: other creditors (not depositors) had not received another 628.2 million dinars as of 30.06.2026 ([AOD report, Q2 2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/kvartalniizvestaj/Nova%20Agrobanka_II%20kvartal%202026.pdf)).
- **Razvojna banka Vojvodine, 06.04.2013.** The National Bank speaks of a transfer of "part of the assets and liabilities" ([notice](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=6375)). According to the press, this was about 17.5 billion dinars of all deposits and about 70,000 depositors ([Kurir](https://biznis.kurir.rs/info-biz/732441/vlasnici-racuna-rbv-raspolazu-svojim-novcem)). In 2015 the National Bank wrote about the transfer of insured and uninsured deposits ([notice](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=8002&konverzija=no)). Access from 08.04. The gap was paid by Serbia and Vojvodina ([AOD report for 2013](https://web.archive.org/web/20161116045606/http://aod.rs/pdf/godisnji_finansijski_izvestaj_za_2013.pdf)). In bankruptcy all creditors received 100%, and the remaining 1.47 billion dinars went to the owners of capital in 2024 ([AOD report, Q3 2024](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/kvartalniizvestaj/614_kvartalni_izvestaj.pdf)). Bankruptcy about 11 years.
- **Privredna banka Beograd, 26.10.2013.** The National Bank speaks of a transfer of "part of the assets and liabilities" ([notice](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=6817)). According to the Ministry of Finance, all deposits, insured and uninsured, were transferred ([Danas](https://www.danas.rs/vesti/ekonomija/postanska-stedionica-preuzima-klijente-pbb-a/); also retold by [UniCredit, web archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20181004145330/http://gss.unicreditgroup.eu/markets/serbia/newsflashes/state-merge-privredna-banka-beograd-banka-postanska-stedionica)). Access from 28.10. The gap was covered by the Republic with bonds, about 11.16 billion dinars, non-repayable ([AOD report for 2013](https://web.archive.org/web/20161116045606/http://aod.rs/pdf/godisnji_finansijski_izvestaj_za_2013.pdf)). By 2025 all creditors, including subordinated debt, had received 100% ([AOD report, Q3 2025](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/kvartalniizvestaj/711_kvartalni_izvestaj.pdf)). Bankruptcy about 12 years.
- **Univerzal banka, 31.01.2014.** The only case in 2012–2018 without a transfer: AOD paid only the insured amount, the rest goes through bankruptcy ([National Bank notice](https://www.nbs.rs/en/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=7067)). According to the National Bank, unlike in earlier cases Poštanska only pays out the insured deposits ([Politika](https://www.politika.rs/sr/clanak/284056/Jednima-majka-drugima-maceha)). The 2012 transfer law applied only to state-controlled banks (art. 1, [text of the law](https://www.nbs.rs/export/sites/NBS_site/documents/propisi/zakoni/kpb_banke_preuzimanje.pdf)), and in the National Bank's banking sector report for Q4 2013 Univerzal was a privately owned bank. We found no official explanation of the choice.
- **Jugbanka, 02.04.2018.** Poštanska took over the bank's assets and liabilities under an agreement between the two banks, on the basis of a Government conclusion ([National Bank notice](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=12646)); according to the National Bank, depositors used their accounts without interruption. Amounts above the limit were not reported separately. Who covered the gap is not visible from the documents. Bankruptcy about 5 years. Other creditors (not depositors) were left without payment on 8.69 billion dinars ([AOD report](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/kvartalniizvestaj/492_kvartalni_izvestaj.pdf)).
- There have been no licence revocations since 2019.

Insurance limit: 5,000 dinars (about €60) until 2005, €3,000 from 2005 (Deposit Insurance Law, Official Gazette 61/05), €50,000 from 2008 (amendments, Official Gazette 116/08, [National Bank](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=3067&konverzija=no)); sole traders, small and medium-sized companies have also been insured since 2008 ([AOD monograph](https://www.aod.rs/downloads/aod_monografija.pdf)).
