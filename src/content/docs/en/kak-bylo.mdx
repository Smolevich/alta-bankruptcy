## In short

- Insurance up to the limit was paid quickly in all past cases where this is known: 1 to 7 days after the licence was revoked.
- In 2012 to 2018, in five of six cases deposits were transferred to another bank (to Nova Agrobanka, later to Poštanska štedionica) and depositors lost nothing. The National Bank has not announced such a transfer for ALTA: as with Univerzal banka in 2014, AOD will itself pay the insured amount up to the limit, on application.
- At Univerzal, individuals, sole traders, small and medium-sized companies received the first 50% above the limit after 6.6 years. By court decisions 100% of recognised rank I claims was distributed (50%, 40% and 10%, without interest for the years of waiting); about 96.5% has actually been paid, and the remaining 72.8 million dinars sit in AOD's reserve.
- Large companies (rank IV) at Univerzal had received about 17.5% by 06.2026, and the case is still open.
- We found no lawsuits by depositors against AOD or the state for amounts above the limit.

## Univerzal banka 2014: who received what

Officially announced (AOD and the National Bank, 31.10.2026): the bank is going into bankruptcy, AOD itself pays the insured amount up to €50,000 to depositors through a bank it will name, and the rest is filed as a claim in the bankruptcy. No transfer of all deposits to another bank, as with Poštanska štedionica in 2012 to 2018, has been announced. The same happened with Univerzal banka in 2014; court deadlines, the size of the estate and the final percentages at ALTA may differ: Univerzal is only a reference point. The licence was revoked on 31.01.2014, bankruptcy was opened on 03.02.2014 (case "1 St 19/2014"), the administrator is AOD. Data as of Q2 2026 from AOD reports; claims recognised for 9,383 million dinars.

> «Агенција за осигурање депозита започеће исплату осигураних износа депонентима Алта банке а.д. Београд најкасније седам радних дана од доношења решења Привредног суда у Београду о отварању стечајног поступка над банком» — [AOD, 31.10.2026](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/137).
>
> «Štediše ove banke biće blagovremeno, u što kraćem roku, obaveštene kod koje banke će moći da podignu svoje depozite do osiguranog iznosa od 50.000 evra, kao i o načinu prijave ostalih svojih potraživanja i o njihovoj isplati» — [Narodna banka Srbije, 31.10.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21851).

- **Insured depositors, about 34,500 people.** Up to €50,000: payout from 04.02.2014 through Poštanska, about €85 million. By the end of 2014, 97 to 98% of the amount due had been paid; the rest collected later (3-year deadline).
- **Rank I** (individuals, sole traders, small companies, amount above the limit; 1,748 creditors). 2,087 million dinars recognised. By court decisions 100% was distributed: 50% (decision of 18.08.2020, paid from 09.2020, after 6.6 years), 40% (decision of 07.04.2021) and 10% (decision of 15.06.2021). About 96.5% has actually been paid; the other 72.8 million dinars are held in AOD's reserve "for payment of remaining obligations", and payment needs the creditor's account details.
- **Ranks II and III** (taxes, National Bank): 100%.
- **Rank IV** (deposits of large companies and other deposits): 6,525 million dinars recognised, about 17.5% paid.
- **Rank V** (other creditors) and **VI** (subordinated debt): nothing.

A typical rank I creditor: median about €53; 1,401 of about 1,750 had less than €1,000. Before all ranks, AOD recovered all the insurance it paid out of the bank's assets, by 2019.

Money above the limit went to those who themselves sent AOD their details (passport, account number; heirs sent documents).

## What "the case is still open" means (12.7 years)

On 30.06.2026 the estate held 2,444.6 million dinars at liquidation value: cash 252 million, real estate 152 million, shares 409 million, debts owed by debtors 313 million, other 1,225 million. Procedure costs are about 35 million dinars a quarter; disputes over contested claims remain. If everything were sold at book value, rank IV would receive at most about 54% of the recognised amount.

## Other cases

- **2002.** Beogradska, Beobanka, Investbanka, Jugobanka. Payout in 7 days through Poštanska; individuals got their whole deposit back except the old foreign-currency savings. The Beogradska banka and Jugobanka bankruptcies are still open (24 years); Investbanka was closed in 2025 after 23 years.
- **Agrobanka, 25.05.2012.** Deposits moved to the successor bank Nova Agrobanka. Bankruptcy closed by court decision in 2023; the remaining assets went to shareholders, so creditors were paid in full.
- **Nova Agrobanka, 27.10.2012.** All deposits, including those above the limit, moved to Poštanska; access from 29.10, no losses for depositors.
- **Razvojna banka Vojvodine, 06.04.2013.** Deposits moved to Poštanska, access from 08.04, no losses, bankruptcy about 11 years.
- **Privredna banka Beograd, 26.10.2013.** Deposits moved to Poštanska, access from 28.10, bankruptcy about 12 years.
- **Univerzal banka, 31.01.2014.** The only case where insurance was paid on application rather than by transferring deposits.
- **Jugbanka, 02.04.2018.** Deposits taken over by Poštanska without interruption; bankruptcy about 5 years.
- There have been no licence revocations since 2019.

Insurance limit: 5,000 dinars (about €60) until 2005, €3,000 from 2005, €50,000 from 2008; sole traders, small and medium-sized companies have also been insured since 2008 ([AOD monograph](https://www.aod.rs/downloads/aod_monografija.pdf)).
