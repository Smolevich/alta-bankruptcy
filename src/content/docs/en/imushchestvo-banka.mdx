## All assets at 30.06.2026

Total assets: RSD 214,931 million (≈ €1,831 million). Liabilities: RSD 187,063 million (≈ €1,594 million), of which deposits and other liabilities to customers RSD 172,045 million (≈ €1,466 million) ([p. 12](/docs/alta-2026-h1-objavljivanje.pdf#page=12)).

Loans to customers and cash on hand and at the National Bank make up 88% of assets. Buildings, land, equipment and subsidiaries together: 3.5%.

- **Cash on hand at 31.12.2025: RSD 53,817 million (≈ €459 million)**: 38,931 million in dinars and 14,886 million in foreign currency (note 17, [p. 94](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=94)). A year earlier: 41,572 million (same page).
- The balance sheet line «Cash and funds at the central bank» at 31.12.2025: RSD 90,497 million ([balance sheet, p. 1](/docs/alta-2025-bilans-stanja.pdf#page=1)); at 30.06.2026: 83,467 million ([p. 12](/docs/alta-2026-h1-objavljivanje.pdf#page=12)).
- Securities at 31.12.2025: RSD 1,087 million, of which Serbian government bonds and treasury bills 923 million (note 18, [p. 94](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=94)).

## How the assets will be valued in bankruptcy

Under the law ([art. 16](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)):

- the inventory and the opening bankruptcy balance sheet show every asset at two values, book and liquidation (para. 3);
- the liquidation value is assessed by a court expert or a licensed auditor (para. 3);
- together with the inventory AOD sends the court a report on the bank's assets, including investments in other companies, and on the bank's significant transactions in the 180 days before bankruptcy (para. 2);
- every quarter AOD updates and publishes on its website a report on the state of the bankruptcy estate (para. 5).

The figures on this page are book values from the bank's reports. No liquidation value is in any published document as of 05.11.2026.
