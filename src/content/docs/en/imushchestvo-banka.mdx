## All assets at 30.06.2026

Total assets were RSD 214,931 million (≈ €1,831 million). Deposits and other customer funds on the same date: RSD 172,045 million (≈ €1,466 million) ([p. 12](/docs/alta-2026-h1-objavljivanje.pdf#page=12)).

<BankAssets lang="en" />

Almost nine tenths of the assets are loans to customers and cash. Buildings, land and subsidiaries together are under 4%.

## Cash

- **Cash on hand at 31.12.2025: RSD 53,817 million (≈ €459 million)**: 38,931 million in dinars and 14,886 million in foreign currency (note 17, [p. 94](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=94)). A year earlier: 41,572 million.
- All cash including accounts at the National Bank at 31.12.2025: RSD 90,166 million (same page); at 30.06.2026: 83,467 million ([p. 12](/docs/alta-2026-h1-objavljivanje.pdf#page=12)).

## Real estate

At 31.12.2025, note 22 ([p. 100](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=100)–[101](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=101)):

| What | RSD m | ≈ € m |
|---|---|---|
| Own buildings | 770.7 | 6.6 |
| Land | 617.7 | 5.3 |
| Leased premises and cars (right of use) | 759.1 | 6.5 |
| Equipment | 656.9 | 5.6 |
| **Total fixed assets** | **2,804.3** | **23.9** |

- Buildings and land were valued by an independent valuer at 31.12.2025; there are no mortgages on the buildings ([p. 101](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=101)).
- Leases of the bank's premises (right of use): RSD 755.0 million, up from 172.0 million a year earlier (same page). These are someone else's premises: they cannot be sold.
- Property let to tenants: 102.6 million, part of an office building (247 m²) and a flat (note 23, same page).
- Head office: Bulevar Zorana Đinđića 121, New Belgrade, plus 17 branches ([p. 13](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=13)). The report does not list which buildings and plots belong to the bank.
- At 30.06.2026 buildings, land and equipment stood at RSD 4,534 million ([p. 12](/docs/alta-2026-h1-objavljivanje.pdf#page=12)), up 1,730 million (≈ €14.7 million) in six months ([balance sheet at 31.12.2025](/docs/alta-2025-bilans-stanja.pdf)). The half-year report does not say what was added.

## Recent purchases and deals

- **ALTA banka Bitola (North Macedonia).** On 16.10.2025 the bank bought 88.29% of Stopanska banka Bitola and raised its stake to 93.29% by the year end. Investment: RSD 2,608.3 million (≈ €22.2 million) ([p. 13](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=13), [p. 100](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=100)).
- **ALTA Leasing (formerly Vantage Leasing), Belgrade.** On 11.07.2024 the bank bought 100%; investment RSD 273.8 million (≈ €2.3 million) (same pages).
- **Loans to related parties**: the bank's owner, its management and other parties related to the bank (note 32; net of provisions):

| At | RSD m | ≈ € m |
|---|---|---|
| 31.12.2023 | 1,014.9 | 8.7 |
| 31.12.2024 | 6,400.0 | 54.7 |
| 31.12.2025 | 10,984.3 | 93.7 |

  Of the 10,984.3 million at 31.12.2025: 2,260.9 million are loans to the shareholder (the bank's sole shareholder is ALTA GROUP doo), 127.1 million to management, 8,596.3 million to "other related parties", whom the report does not name ([p. 113](/docs/alta-2025-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=113); 2023 from the [2024 report, p. 111](/docs/alta-2024-izvestaj-revizora.pdf#page=111)).
- **Large exposures** (each above 10% of the bank's capital) totalled 293.48% of capital at the end of 2025; the bank's own limit in that report is 380% ([2025 disclosures, p. 22](/docs/alta-2025-objavljivanje.pdf#page=22)).

## What may come back to depositors, and what probably will not

<p class="assets-guess">Our assessment, not figures from the reports and not a payout forecast.</p>

<div class="assets-odds">
<div>
<h3>Most likely to turn into money</h3>
<ul>
<li>Cash on hand, at the National Bank and at other banks, but only what was left when bankruptcy opened. AOD's inventory will show how much.</li>
<li>Serbian government bonds: easy to sell.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="mid">
<h3>Depends on borrowers and buyers</h3>
<ul>
<li>Loans to customers, the largest item. They are repaid on schedule over years or sold at a discount.</li>
<li>Loans to related parties: depends on whether those companies can pay.</li>
<li>Buildings and land: at the sale price, which may differ from the valuation in the report.</li>
<li>ALTA banka Bitola and ALTA Leasing: if a buyer is found.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="low">
<h3>Probably worth little</h3>
<ul>
<li>Right of use of leased premises: not the bank's property.</li>
<li>The bank's software and licences (RSD 443 million).</li>
<li>Tax and other accounting assets.</li>
</ul>
</div>

On paper, at 30.06.2026 the assets (214,931 million) exceeded customer funds (172,045 million). In bankruptcy assets usually sell for less than their book value, and the procedure itself costs money. How it went at Univerzal banka: see [«How it went with Univerzal»](/en/kak-bylo/).

## Open questions

1. **The plot under the head office.** In 2025 the bank commissioned the design of a 35-storey tower in Block 32 on the site of its current building: the lower floors for the new head office, flats and offices above ([N1, 07.10.2025](https://n1info.rs/biznis/alta-banka-kula-novi-beograd-zaha-hadid/); [Biznis.rs, 07.10.2025](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/alta-banka-gradi-soliter-od-35-spratova-na-novom-beogradu/)). In June 2026 Biznis.rs reported that a plan for the site was being prepared for an "Alta kula" of up to 125 m for the company Alta Real Estate ([Biznis.rs, 30.06.2026](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/pripremljen-planski-osnov-za-alta-kulu-kod-merkatora-visoku-125-metara/)). Who owns the plot and the building, the bank (then they are part of the bankruptcy estate) or another company, the bank's reports do not say. AOD's inventory and the cadastre will answer it.
2. **Fixed assets up RSD 1,730 million in H1 2026.** What was bought or leased is not explained in the half-year report.
3. **Who the "other related parties" are**, with loans of RSD 8,596.3 million at 31.12.2025. The report gives only the total.
