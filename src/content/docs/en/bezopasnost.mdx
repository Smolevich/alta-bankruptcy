After a licence is revoked, people turn up in chats and private messages offering help in return for money or your personal data. Below is what the authorities have said, and what we advise. Where it says “advice”, it is our opinion, not a rule of law.

## Nobody can buy you a place in line

Nobody except AOD and the court puts you on a list or changes the order of payments. AOD pays the insured amount up to €50,000 to all insured depositors by law, and the list of claims above the limit is checked by AOD as administrator and then by the court ([art. 17 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). The order of payments is set by law ([art. 20](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)), and nobody can buy a place in it.

- “We will speed up the payout”, “we will put you on the list”, “we will get it back faster for a percentage”: AOD, the National Bank and the court have announced no such services.
- AOD, the National Bank and the court do not offer to buy claims. If someone offers to buy your claim, do not sign anything or send any documents until your lawyer has read the contract.
- Official announcements come from AOD ([aod.rs](https://www.aod.rs/)), the National Bank ([nbs.rs](https://www.nbs.rs/)) and the Commercial Court in Belgrade; the bankruptcy notice is published in the Official Gazette, Službeni glasnik ([art. 7 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). Check any message against these sources.

> «Обавештење o условима, местима и роковима исплате осигураних износа депозита биће објављено у средствима јавног информисања и на веб сајту Агенције за осигурање депозита (www.aod.rs) најкасније три радна дана по доношењу решења Привредног суда у Београду о отварању стечајног поступка над банком» — [AOD, 31.10.2026](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/137).

## What not to send
Advice: do not send your passport, bank agreement, statements, account number, JMBG or card photos in chats, private messages or to “organisers”. Exceptions: a lawyer you have signed a contract with, and AOD or the court themselves, through the official channels they announce. For the payout of up to €50,000 you will show your documents at a branch of the paying bank, Banka Poštanska štedionica, once AOD announces the date and branches ([AOD, 03.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/139)).

**ALTA loan: do not pay via SMS.** On 04.11.2026 the National Bank warned of fraudulent SMS from “AltaPay” and from unknown numbers asking ALTA borrowers to repay their loans ([NBS, 04.11.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21866)). AOD will publish the account of ALTA in bankruptcy for loan payments on aod.rs ([AOD, questions and answers on ALTA](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/pitanja_odgovori_altabanka)).

## The insurance payout is free

> «Загарантовано осигурање депозита је бесплатно за депоненте» — [AOD, questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori).

AOD’s announcement of 31.10.2026 mentions no fee for the payout. Advice: a request to pay a “commission” or “fee” for the insurance payout is a reason to stop and check on aod.rs.

## If someone writes “from AOD”

Advice: do not reply to the message; find the contact yourself on the [AOD contacts page](https://www.aod.rs/clan/hpstart/contact) (Masarikova 5, Belgrade) and call or write there. According to AOD, the conditions and dates of the payout are announced in the media and on aod.rs, not in private messages.

## How to check a lawyer

If a “lawyer” writes to you from an unfamiliar Telegram profile, first check that they are in the official register (directory) of a bar association.

1. Look the person up in the bar directory: where and how to search, and how to see their cases in the courts’ open databases, is on the page [A lawyer: why, how to choose and how to check](/en/advokat/#kak-proverit).
2. A matching name is not enough. Compare the city, the office address and the phone number from the register, and call that number yourself, not the one given in Telegram. We compared 16 “lawyer” profiles from Instagram with the register: 10 were in it, and for 4 of them the details in the profile did not match the register. This is not necessarily fraud, but a reason to ask again. Hashtags and captions such as “lawyer Belgrade” prove nothing.
3. A “jurist”, “legal adviser” or a residence-permit agency will not be in the lawyers’ register, and that is normal. But such a person cannot represent you in court in the bankruptcy case and is not bound by attorney–client privilege. An agency with “lawyer” on its sign is a company; lawyers appear in the register by their surnames.
4. Advice: a real lawyer works under a written contract or power of attorney (punomoćje) and does not ask for an advance payment to a private person’s card.
5. Not in the register, or the contacts do not match: do not send documents.

## Where to complain

- A complaint about a bank: the National Bank’s Financial Consumer Protection Department, zastita.korisnika@nbs.rs, free Info Centre 0800 111 110 ([National Bank page](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/ciljevi-i-funkcije/zastita-korisnika/), [contacts by unit](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/o_nbs/kontakti-prema-organizacionoj-strukturi/)).
- Fraud: the police, number 192 ([Serbian Ministry of Interior site](https://www.mup.gov.rs/)). We did not find a separate contact for the high-tech crime unit on official sites and do not publish one.
- If you have already sent your data: tell the banks where you hold accounts now, and the police.
