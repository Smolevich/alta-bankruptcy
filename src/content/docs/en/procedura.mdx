**Two processes are running at once:** from 13.11.2026 Poštanska pays out up to €50,000 (apply by 03.11.2029); the claim for the amount above €50,000 is due by 04.02.2027 ([where this deadline comes from](/en/trebovanie/#deadline)).

**How we got here.** On 03.11.2026 the Commercial Court in Belgrade opened bankruptcy. The administrator is AOD, the paying bank is Banka Poštanska štedionica ([AOD](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/139)). On 05.11 Poštanska and AOD signed the payout contract ([Poštanska, 05.11.2026](https://www.posted.co.rs/media/novosti.html)). On 06.11 AOD announced that the payout of up to €50,000 starts on 13.11.2026 at the Poštanska branches designated for ALTA depositors; the deadline to apply is 03.11.2029 ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)). AOD published the branch list on 06.11 ([PDF](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Spisak.pdf)). You can collect the money within three years.

**The key point: AOD will start paying insured deposits of up to €50,000 no later than 7 working days after the court decides to open bankruptcy.** (Our calculation: the court decided on 03.11.2026, so for ALTA by 13.11.2026.) This is what AOD and the National Bank announced on 31.10.2026: the bank goes into bankruptcy, AOD itself pays insured deposits through a paying bank, and anything above that is filed as a claim in the bankruptcy. Nobody has announced a transfer of all deposits to another bank, as happened with Poštanska štedionica in 2012–2018.

## 1. The National Bank revokes the licence

ALTA: 30.10.2026. Univerzal: 31.01.2014.

## 2. The court opened bankruptcy

By law the court opens bankruptcy no later than the next working day after it receives the National Bank's decision ([art. 6](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)); AOD becomes the bankruptcy administrator ([art. 8 para. 2](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). For ALTA the Commercial Court in Belgrade decided on 03.11.2026. The case number is 10 St 275/2026, confirmed by the APR decision of 03.11.2026 entering the bankruptcy in the register ([how to check](#check-case)). The legal effects of bankruptcy start on the day the notice is posted on the court's notice board ([art. 9](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)).

At Univerzal: 3 days later, on Monday 03.02.2014.

## 3. AOD named the paying bank

For ALTA: 03.11.2026, the bank is Poštanska štedionica. By law, no later than 3 working days after the court decision ([Law on Deposit Insurance, art. 16 para. 8](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)).

At Univerzal: announced the same day as the court decision; the paying bank was Poštanska štedionica ([AOD announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20140308161705/http://www.aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-03-saopstenje.pdf)).

## 4. Payout up to €50,000: we are here

No later than 7 working days after the court decision; for ALTA by 13.11.2026 (11.11 is a public holiday, [Law on State and Other Holidays, arts. 1a and 3](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2001/43/1/reg)). For ALTA, Poštanska signed the contract with AOD on 05.11; on 06.11 AOD announced that the payout starts on 13.11.2026, deadline to apply 03.11.2029 ([AOD notice](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)). You can collect it within 3 years ([art. 16 paras. 8 and 9](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)).

AOD has confirmed in writing: an individual and the same person as a sole trader each get their own €50,000 limit; transfers go only to an account at a bank in Serbia, and sole traders are paid by transfer only. Details and screenshots of the letters: [AOD's written answers](/en/vyplata/#aod-answers).

At Univerzal: from the day after the court decision, 04.02.2014, at the branches of the paying bank, Poštanska štedionica, on the depositor’s application (this is not the claim from step 5).

## 5. Claim for the amount above €50,000

Claim for the amount above €50,000 — by **4 February 2027**. [Where this deadline comes from →](/en/trebovanie/#deadline) The claim is filed in writing ([Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). File without waiting for the insurance payout. Details are on the page [Claim above €50,000](/en/trebovanie/).

At Univerzal: the court gave 60 days ([notice in the Official Gazette](https://web.archive.org/web/20160830214352/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-10-sluzbeni-glasnik-univerzal-banka.pdf); the same Gazette page has notices naming private persons, so we do not keep a copy and the link goes to the web archive).

## 6. AOD verifies the claims, the court approves the list

AOD verifies claims for up to 6 months after the filing deadline ends; then, within 60 days at the latest, the court holds a hearing and draws up the final list ([art. 17 paras. 1 and 2](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). How much the bank owes depositors above the limit should become clear about 8 to 12 months after bankruptcy is opened.

Within 60 days of the opening AOD takes an inventory of the assets and draws up the opening balance, then publishes a quarterly report on aod.rs ([art. 16](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). The creditors' committee has 5 members (exceptionally more, on a reasoned proposal by AOD), chosen by the judge on AOD's proposal, taking claim sizes into account ([art. 8](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)); a bank bankruptcy has no creditors' assembly ([art. 22](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)).

Before any rank is paid, the bank's assets cover the costs of the procedure, unpaid wages and the insurance AOD paid out, which counts as a procedure cost ([art. 19 para. 1 item 6](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)).

**Deadlines from the court decision on ALTA** (10 St 275/2026 of 03.11.2026, published on the court notice board):

## 7. Payouts above €50,000

The law sets no deadline: money is paid as the bank's assets are sold. Individuals, sole traders, micro, small and medium-sized companies rank first ([art. 20 item 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)) and are paid in proportion to their claims ([Law on Bankruptcy, art. 54 para. 3](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

How it went at Univerzal: rank I received about 96.5% of recognised claims after 6 years 7 months to 7 years 4 months, with no interest for the years of waiting; the case is still open, almost 13 years on. Details and primary sources are on the [Past cases](/en/kak-bylo/#univerzal-sources) page.
