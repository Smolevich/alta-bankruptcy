What is official so far (AOD and the National Bank, 31.10.2026): the bank goes into bankruptcy. AOD itself pays insured deposits up to €50,000 through a bank it will name. Anything above that is filed as a claim in the bankruptcy. Nobody has announced a transfer of all deposits to another bank, as happened with Poštanska štedionica in 2012–2018.

> «Агенција за осигурање депозита започеће исплату осигураних износа депонентима Алта банке а.д. Београд најкасније седам радних дана од доношења решења Привредног суда у Београду о отварању стечајног поступка над банком» — [AOD, 31.10.2026](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/137).
>
> Translation: “AOD will start paying out insured amounts to depositors of Alta banka no later than seven working days after the decision of the Commercial Court in Belgrade opening bankruptcy.”
>
> «Štediše ove banke biće blagovremeno, u što kraćem roku, obaveštene kod koje banke će moći da podignu svoje depozite do osiguranog iznosa od 50.000 evra, kao i o načinu prijave ostalih svojih potraživanja i o njihovoj isplati» — [Narodna banka Srbije, 31.10.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21851).
>
> Translation: “Depositors of this bank will be notified in good time and as soon as possible at which bank they can withdraw their deposits up to the insured amount of EUR 50,000, as well as how to file their other claims and how they will be paid.”

The deadlines below come from the laws. For comparison, the nearest case is Univerzal banka (2014), where it went the same way. Court deadlines, the size of the bankruptcy estate (what the bank has left to sell) and the final percentages at ALTA may differ: treat Univerzal as a guide only. More on the page [Past cases](/en/kak-bylo/).

## 1. The National Bank revokes the licence

ALTA: 30.10.2026, a Friday. Univerzal: 31.01.2014, also a Friday.

## 2. The court opens bankruptcy: we are here

By law the court opens bankruptcy no later than the next working day after it receives the National Bank's decision ([art. 6](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)); AOD becomes the bankruptcy administrator ([art. 8 para. 2](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). For ALTA there is no court decision as of 01.11.2026.

At Univerzal: 3 days later, on Monday 03.02.2014.

## 3. AOD announces which bank pays

No later than 3 working days after the court decision ([Law on Deposit Insurance, art. 16 para. 8](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)).

At Univerzal: announced the same day as the court decision; the paying bank was Poštanska štedionica ([AOD announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20140308161705/http://www.aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-03-saopstenje.pdf)).

## 4. Payout up to €50,000

No later than 7 working days after the court decision. You can collect it within 3 years ([art. 16 paras. 8 and 9](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)).

AOD has confirmed in writing: an individual and the same person as a sole trader each get their own €50,000 limit; transfers go only to an account at a bank in Serbia, and sole traders are paid by transfer only. Details and screenshots of the letters: [AOD's written answers](/en/trebovanie/#aod-otvety).

At Univerzal: from the day after the court decision, 04.02.2014, at the branches of the paying bank, Poštanska štedionica, on the depositor’s application (this is not the claim from step 5).

## 5. Claim for the amount above €50,000

The court sets the deadline: 30 to 90 days from publication in the Official Gazette ([art. 7](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg); filing in writing: [Law on Bankruptcy, art. 111](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). File without waiting for the insurance payout. Details are on the page [Claim above €50,000](/en/trebovanie/).

At Univerzal: the court gave 60 days ([notice in the Official Gazette](https://web.archive.org/web/20160830214352/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-10-sluzbeni-glasnik-univerzal-banka.pdf)).

## 6. AOD verifies the claims, the court approves the list

AOD verifies claims for up to 6 months after the filing deadline ends; then, within 60 days at the latest, the court holds a hearing and draws up the final list ([art. 17 paras. 1 and 2](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). How much the bank owes depositors above the limit should become clear about 8 to 12 months after bankruptcy is opened.

Before any rank is paid, the bank's assets cover the costs of the procedure, unpaid wages and the insurance AOD paid out, which counts as a procedure cost ([art. 19 para. 1 item 6](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)).

## 7. Payouts above €50,000

The law sets no deadline: money is paid as the bank's assets are sold. Individuals, sole traders, micro, small and medium-sized companies rank first ([art. 20 item 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)) and are paid in proportion to their claims ([Law on Bankruptcy, art. 54 para. 3](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

At Univerzal: the first 50% came after 6 years and 7 months (09.2020). Over 6.5 to 7.5 years the court ordered 100% of recognised rank I claims to be paid out, in three parts (50%, 40% and 10%) and with no interest for the years of waiting. About 96.5% has actually reached creditors; the rest is held in AOD's reserve ([AOD report](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/kvartalniizvestaj/Univerzal%20banka_II%20kvartal%202026.pdf)). The case is still open, 12.7 years on. [Primary sources: distribution decisions and AOD reports](/en/kak-bylo/#univerzal-sources).
