The payout is calculated from the balance on the day bankruptcy was opened (03.11.2026), with interest up to that day ([Law on Deposit Insurance, art. 16 para. 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)). So you need both the contracts and the balances with dates.

### What to do without statements

- Gather what you already have: contracts, ALTA statement emails in your mailbox ([how to keep an email whole](#statement-emails)), bank SMS messages with balances, paper receipts, old PDFs and screenshots.
- For the payout up to €50,000 itself, screenshots and photos of documents are not needed and do not replace the original. AOD requires «оригиналну документацију» (the original documents) for the deposit; without the original, only a certificate from ALTA banka in bankruptcy or a copy certified by its authorised person ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf); [AOD procedure, section VIII, item 6](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)). Screenshots, SMS and old statements help only if your amount does not match the paying bank's calculation, and for the claim above €50,000.
- For the payout up to €50,000 the paying bank needs an ID card or passport (exceptionally another identity document with a photo) and the original deposit document: a contract, a savings book, a «картица текућег динарског или девизног рачуна» (card of a dinar or foreign-currency current account) or another document from the AOD list ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)) ([what to bring](/en/vyplata/)). No contract: [what to do](/en/vyplata/#no-contract).
- Keep any unused ALTA cheques: they are handed in at ALTA banka in bankruptcy, and the settlement confirmation is the basis for paying the withheld part of the insured amount ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf); [details](/en/vyplata/#cheques)).
- For a court claim above €50,000: contracts, account and deposit numbers, and statements with the balance on the latest available date ([how to file a claim](/en/trebovanie/)). Under AOD's instructions of 10.11.2026 the claim comes with copies: savings agreement, bank statement, account card; the bank already shows the contractual interest in the last statement as of 03.11.2026 ([more](/en/trebovanie/#claim-attachments)). You state the amount in the claim yourself: the principal and, separately, the interest; AOD then checks it ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 111](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)).

## Keep statement emails whole

Our advice: keep the monthly emails from ALTA whole, not only the PDF attachment. In Gmail: the message menu “Download message” gives you an .eml file. The original email usually carries the bank's digital signature (DKIM), which confirms the email came from the bank. A PDF attachment on its own does not prove that.

#### What to save

- Your passport, and your residence permit if you have one. A non-resident is paid on a passport or another identity document with a photo ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)).
- Current and foreign-currency account agreements and the deposit agreement; if there is no paper copy, a PDF from your mailbox or a photo (for the payout up to €50,000 these do not replace the original).
- A screenshot of the web banking home page (“Početna”): all accounts with numbers and balances. The most useful picture if nothing else opens.
- Each deposit (“Depoziti”): a screenshot of the whole “Detalji depozita” page, with the deposit number, amount, rate and balance.
- PDF statements for each account, dinar and foreign-currency separately, from the day the account was opened or at least for 2026.
- Transactions for October (“Promet po računu”, 01.10–31.10): they are not in any monthly statement yet.
- Account details (“Detalji računa”), cards (“Kartice”) and loans (“Krediti”): keep the loan balance, because a loan is not forgiven in bankruptcy.
- Screenshots with the date visible (the phone or computer clock in the frame). Keep them off your phone: in the cloud or your mailbox.

#### If you are a sole trader or a company

Sole traders and companies log in differently, and many have no web access. Below is depositors' experience, not a bank instruction: it is not confirmed that login, synchronisation and export still work after the licence was revoked. If it does not go through, save at least a screenshot of the screen with your accounts.

##### Corporate web banking

Address: [online.altabanka.rs/Corporate](https://online.altabanka.rs/Corporate). It was enabled at a branch, so many do not have it. Easy to check: look for a personal/business profile switch in the app. If you have it, do the same as for personal web banking: the code from the app (business profile), log in, statements for each account.

##### OfficeBanking and a card reader

You need a Windows computer: put the “eBanking” card into the USB reader, open ALTA OfficeBanking and enter the card PIN. After logging in press “Synchronise” (“Синхронизовать”) at once, or the program shows balances as of your last login.

## Do not send statements to anyone

Do not send statements, contracts or screenshots to anyone except your lawyer and AOD through an official channel. More: [how to avoid scams](/en/bezopasnost/).
