**The ALTA web banking may stop working at any moment. Save your statements now.** You will need them if the paying bank's calculation does not match and for a claim above €50,000. The steps below come from depositors' experience and may go out of date: the bank's servers sometimes respond and sometimes do not.

The payout is calculated from the balance on the day the court opens bankruptcy, with interest up to that day ([Law on Deposit Insurance, art. 16 para. 1](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)). So you need both the contracts and the balances with dates.

## What to save

- Your passport, and your residence permit if you have one. The paying bank needs a photo ID ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)).
- Current and foreign-currency account agreements and the deposit agreement; if there is no paper copy, a PDF from your mailbox or a photo.
- A screenshot of the web banking home page (“Početna”): all accounts with numbers and balances. The most useful picture if nothing else opens.
- Each deposit (“Depoziti”): a screenshot of the whole “Detalji depozita” page, with the deposit number, amount, rate and balance.
- PDF statements for each account, dinar and foreign-currency separately, from the day the account was opened or at least for 2026.
- Transactions for October (“Promet po računu”, 01.10–31.10): they are not in any monthly statement yet.
- Account details (“Detalji računa”), cards (“Kartice”) and loans (“Krediti”): keep the loan balance, because a loan is not forgiven in bankruptcy.
- Screenshots with the date visible (the phone or computer clock in the frame). Keep them off your phone: in the cloud or your mailbox.

## How to export statements from the web banking

1. Find your login (“Korisničko ime”). In the ALTA mobile app: profile (person icon), the “My data” tab, the “Username” field. Do not confuse it with “User's Full Name” (a name with a space): the login is “Username”.
2. Get the code without logging in to the app: the “Token” tab at the bottom, “OTP Authorization”. An 8-digit one-time code appears and is valid for 10 minutes.
3. Open [online.altabanka.rs/Retail](https://online.altabanka.rs/Retail). Enter your login, and in the password field (“mToken”) the code from step 2, then press “Prijavi se”.
4. On the left “Računi”, “Izvodi po računu”. Choose the account at the top (dinar and foreign-currency separately). In “Po datumu” set the period and save the “PDF”. About 6 months are returned at a time, so export in pieces.
5. A section may answer “Dogodila se nepoznata greška”: wait a minute and open it again. A PDF sometimes lands in “Downloads” without a download window.

If you have no access: look for ALTA emails in your mailbox (statements go to the e-mail registered at the bank), save the bank's SMS messages about balances and paper receipts. Bank support: +381 11 22 05 500, info@altabanka.rs (may not answer now).

## Keep statement emails whole

Our advice: keep the monthly emails from ALTA whole, not only the PDF attachment. In Gmail: the message menu “Download message” gives you an .eml file. The original email usually carries the bank's digital signature (DKIM), which confirms the email came from the bank. A PDF attachment on its own does not prove that.

## If you are a sole trader or a company

Web banking for sole traders and companies is different, and many have no web access. According to depositors, there are two routes, neither confirmed since the licence was revoked:

- The web login for companies and sole traders [online.altabanka.rs/Corporate](https://online.altabanka.rs/Corporate): it was enabled at a branch. Easy to check: look for a personal/business profile switch in the app.
- The ALTA OfficeBanking program with the “eBanking” card and a reader: after logging in press “Synchronise” (“Синхронизовать”) at once, or it shows balances as of your last login. If synchronisation does not go through, save at least a screenshot of the screen with your accounts.

Your accountant may have had access too: ask them to export statements as well. Save the same as for personal accounts: statements for each account, October transactions, balances, loans and unexecuted payments.

## Do not send statements to anyone

Do not send statements, contracts or screenshots to anyone except your lawyer and AOD through an official channel. More: [how to avoid scams](/en/bezopasnost/).
