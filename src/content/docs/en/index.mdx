The National Bank of Serbia revoked ALTA bank's licence on 30.10.2026. The state Deposit Insurance Agency (AOD) will repay up to €50,000 per person. Everything above that is recovered through the bank's bankruptcy: it takes a long time, and you must file the claim yourself. This site is for people who have more than €50,000 at ALTA.

## Three steps if you have more than €50,000

<div class="steps3">
<div>

### 1. What to do now

Keep everything that proves your balance: account and deposit agreements, account numbers, statements showing the balance, your passport. Watch [aod.rs](https://www.aod.rs/) for the announcement: it will appear within 3 working days of the court decision. Nothing needs to be filed before that announcement.

<small>More: [what to prepare](/en/trebovanie/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 2. When to file the claim

A claim for the amount above €50,000 is filed in writing with the court handling the bankruptcy. The court sets the deadline: 30 to 90 days from publication of the notice in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik). Do not wait for the insurance payout: the deadline runs in parallel.

<small>More: [claim above €50,000](/en/trebovanie/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 3. What to expect

The size of the bank's debt to depositors above the limit should become clear about 8 to 12 months from now. There is no payout deadline: money is paid as the bank's assets are sold. In the one comparable case, Univerzal banka (2014), the first payment above the limit came after 6.6 years.

<small>More: [how it went with Univerzal](/en/kak-bylo/)</small>

</div>
</div>

## Up to €50,000: AOD pays

This part is paid by the state deposit insurance fund and nothing has to be filed in court: AOD will announce the conditions, the paying bank and the dates. See the [official AOD announcement](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/137) and the [AOD questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori).
