For ALTA depositors with more than €50,000. AOD, the state Deposit Insurance Agency that also administers the bank's bankruptcy, repays up to €50,000. The rest comes back only through the bank's bankruptcy, and you have to file a claim with the court yourself.

<Status lang="en" />

## Three steps if you have more than €50,000

<div class="steps3">
<div>

### 1. <span class="today">Today: save the statements and documents you already have</span>

<aside class="save-tip not-content">
<strong>ALTA web banking no longer works</strong>
<p>The bank in bankruptcy says: «Платне услуге и електронски сервиси банке више нису у функцији и сви рачуни клијената су затворени» (payment and electronic services no longer work and all client accounts are closed; <a href="https://altabanka.rs/">altabanka.rs</a>, checked 05.11.2026). Without web banking, use your agreements, the bank's statement emails and SMS messages about balances: <a href="/en/vypiski/#no-web-banking">what to save without web banking</a>.</p>
</aside>

- PDF statements you managed to download;
- the bank's statement emails and SMS messages about balances;
- your account and deposit agreements.

You don't need to send these documents anywhere on their own — just collect them now. If you have more than €50,000, file your claim with the court by 4 February 2027.

<small>How to export them: [What to save](/en/vypiski/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 2. When to file the claim

Claim for the amount above €50,000 — by **4 February 2027**. [Where this deadline comes from →](/en/trebovanie/#deadline) Don't wait for the €50,000 payout: the deadline runs regardless.

<small>More: [claim above €50,000](/en/trebovanie/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 3. What to expect

How much the bank owes depositors above the limit will be clear after the claims review: AOD has up to 6 months after the filing period ends, then the court holds a hearing no later than 60 days after that ([Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks, art. 17](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). There is no payout deadline: money is paid as the bank's assets are sold. At Univerzal banka (2014) the first payment above the limit came after 6 years 7 months (09.2020). Once payouts above the limit begin, payment details will be needed: before the first distribution at Univerzal (2020), the bank in bankruptcy (administered by AOD) asked creditors to send a copy of their ID card, account number and phone.

<small>More: [how it went with Univerzal](/en/kak-bylo/)</small>

</div>
</div>

<div class="example">

**Example: you have €140,000 at ALTA.** This is an example, not a forecast.

- **€50,000** is paid by AOD; you don't file anything in court for it.
- **€90,000** is your claim in court, filed by 4 February 2027. The money comes as the bank's assets are sold. Univerzal depositors in rank I received about 96.5% of recognised claims, in three parts, after 6 years 7 months to 7 years 4 months, with no interest for the years of waiting ([how it went](/en/kak-bylo/)).

</div>

If you have less than €50,000, AOD repays all of it: you don't file anything in court. The insured amount is paid at Poštanska branches from 13.11.2026 until 03.11.2029 ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)); what to bring: [checklist](/en/vyplata/#what-to-bring).

Why the licence was revoked, in the National Bank's words: the bank's financial condition "deteriorated significantly", threatening the interests of its depositors ([full quote](/en/bank/#why)).

<p class="safe-strip not-content"><strong>Beware of scams.</strong> Do not send your passport or account details to anyone who promises to speed up the payout or buy your claim. Official announcements come from AOD (aod.rs), the National Bank (nbs.rs) and the Commercial Court in Belgrade; the bankruptcy notice is published in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik). <a href="/en/bezopasnost/">How to avoid scams →</a></p>

## What happens after the court decision

The court opened ALTA's bankruptcy on 03.11.2026 (case 10 St 275/2026, [how to check it yourself](/en/procedura/#check-case)). What comes next, by law and by how AOD handled Univerzal banka in 2014:

<div class="next-steps">

1. **Up to €50,000: at a Poštanska branch, from 13.11.2026 until 03.11.2029.** Dinar deposits are paid in dinars, foreign-currency deposits in euros at the middle rate of 03.11.2026 ([AOD, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)). Bring a valid identity document (foreigners: passport) and the original deposit document: contract, savings book or account card. Sole traders, companies and anyone collecting for another person with an authorisation need more documents ([AOD, forms](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka)). If you disagree with the amount, file a complaint with AOD or Poštanska. Checklist for individuals, sole traders, companies, authorised persons and heirs, with ticks and printing: [«What to bring to Poštanska»](/en/vyplata/#what-to-bring).
2. **Above €50,000: a claim to the court by 4 February 2027** ([where this deadline comes from](/en/trebovanie/#deadline)). File it in writing with Privredni sud u Beogradu, Masarikova 2 ([court website](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/)). You can write it on [AOD's template](/en/trebovanie/#claim-form). More: [“Claim”](/en/trebovanie/).
3. **A loan with ALTA: keep paying; a safe-deposit box: wait for AOD.** What clients owe the bank is part of the bank's estate, which the administrator collects ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 101](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). In 2014, three days after the court decision, AOD published the account for Univerzal banka loan payments and how box holders could collect their items ([AOD notice, 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20160830213118/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-06-obavestenje-o-nacinu-izmirivanja.pdf)). For ALTA, AOD announced the account on 06.11: pay to the dinar account of ALTA in bankruptcy held at the NBS, no. 780-282-42 and enter the loan account number in the «poziv na broj» field ([AOD instruction, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo_novi_ra%C4%8Dun_6_11_2026.pdf)). If your loan is with ALTA GROUP DOO Beograd (not the bank): account 200-3408551901000-45 at Poštanska štedionica, loan account number in «poziv na broj»; these are two different legal entities, pay only to these two accounts ([NBS, 06.11.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr_RS/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21884)). Our tip: which of the two entities your loan is with is stated in your loan agreement.
4. **Payments above €50,000: by payment rank, no deadline.** First AOD recovers the insurance it paid out and the procedure costs, then rank I is paid: individuals, sole traders, micro, small and medium companies, all at the same percentage (art. 19–20 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks; art. 54 para. 3 of the Bankruptcy Law). At Univerzal the first payment came after 6 years 7 months. More: [“Procedure”](/en/procedura/).

</div>
