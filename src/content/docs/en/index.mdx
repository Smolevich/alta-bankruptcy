## What happens after the court decision

The court opened ALTA's bankruptcy on 03.11.2026. What comes next, by law and by how AOD handled Univerzal banka in 2014:

<div class="next-steps">

1. **Waiting for AOD's payout notice.** AOD must announce the start date and the branches within 3 working days of the court decision ([Law on Deposit Insurance, art. 16(8)](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/2/reg)). The paying bank is Banka Poštanska štedionica ([AOD, 03.11](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/139)). It signed the contract with AOD on 05.11 ([Poštanska, 05.11.2026](https://www.posted.co.rs/media/novosti.html)). As of the morning of 06.11 there is no date yet. Watch [aod.rs](https://www.aod.rs/).
2. **Up to €50,000: at a Poštanska branch, within 3 years.** Payouts start no later than 7 working days after the court decision, that is by 13.11.2026 (11.11 is a public holiday, [Law on State and Other Holidays, arts. 1a and 3](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2001/43/1/reg)); you can collect the money within 3 years (art. 16(8)–(9)). Dinar deposits are paid in dinars, foreign-currency deposits in euros (art. 17). Bring your ID card (foreigners: passport) and an ALTA document for the deposit: contract, savings book or account card ([AOD procedure, section VIII](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/osigdepoz/pravniokvir/cir/16_pravniokvir.pdf)). Sole traders and companies are paid only by transfer to an account in Serbia. If you disagree with the amount, write to AOD (art. 17(3)). Step by step: [«How to get the insured amount up to €50,000»](/en/vyplata/).
3. **Above €50,000: a claim to the court, 90 days.** The 90 days run from the day the notice appears in the Official Gazette, not from 3 November. The court decision says so (10 St 275/2026, item IV), and so does the law ([Law on Bankruptcy of Banks, art. 7](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). File it in writing with Privredni sud u Beogradu, Masarikova 2 ([court website](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/)). You can write it on [AOD's template](/en/trebovanie/#obrazac). [Where to find the notice](/en/trebovanie/#glasnik). More: [“Claim”](/en/trebovanie/).
4. **A loan with ALTA: keep paying; a safe-deposit box: wait for AOD.** What clients owe the bank is part of the bank's estate, which the administrator collects ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 101](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). In 2014, three days after the court decision, AOD published the account for Univerzal banka loan payments and how box holders could collect their items ([AOD notice, 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20160830213118/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-06-obavestenje-o-nacinu-izmirivanja.pdf)). For ALTA, AOD will announce the details.
5. **First months: inventory and claim review.** Within 60 days AOD takes an inventory of the bank's assets, then publishes a quarterly report on aod.rs (art. 16). AOD reviews claims for up to 6 months after the filing deadline, then within 60 days the court approves the list (art. 17). The creditors' committee has 5 members chosen by the judge on AOD's proposal, taking claim sizes into account; a bank bankruptcy has no creditors' assembly (art. 8 and 22).
6. **Payments above €50,000: by priority class, no deadline.** First AOD recovers the insurance it paid out and the procedure costs, then the first class is paid: individuals, sole traders, micro, small and medium companies, all at the same percentage (art. 19–20). At Univerzal the first payment came after 6 years 7 months. More: [“Procedure”](/en/procedura/).

</div>

<div class="court-case">

**How to check the case at the court yourself.** Case number: **10 St 275/2026** (on the portal you enter Ст / 275 / 2026). Confirmation: the decision of the Business Registers Agency (APR) of 03.11.2026 entering the opening of ALTA's bankruptcy in the register; it cites the court decision opening bankruptcy “Посл.бр. 10 Ст. 275/2026 од 03.11.2026”.

**How to check in the APR register.** Open the [APR search](https://pretraga.apr.gov.rs/search) (“Oбjедињена претрага”), press “Матични број” (registration number), type 07074433 and press Enter. The result is “ALTA BANKA AD BEOGRAD - U STEČAJU”, status “У стечају” (in bankruptcy). Click the name: under “Законски заступници” (legal representatives) you see “РС - Агенција за осигурање депозита”, function “стечајни управник” (bankruptcy administrator); under “Одлуке регистратора” for 2026 there is the APR decision of 03.11.2026 (БДСЛ 18465/2026).

**How to follow the case on the courts portal:**

1. Open the portal [“Ток предмета привредних судова”](https://tpson.portal.sud.rs/tpprvs/) (Tok predmeta privrednih sudova, commercial court case progress). At the top left you can switch “Ћирилица | Latinica”.
2. “Суд” (Sud, court) → БЕОГРАД.
3. “Уписник” (Upisnik, case register) → Ст (St, bankruptcy).
4. “Број предмета” (Broj predmeta, case number) → 275.
5. “Година” (Godina, year) → 2026, then click another field, otherwise the year is reset.
6. Below the fields there is a picture with a sum, e.g. “389 + 96”: add it up and type the answer in the box under the picture.
7. Press “Претрага” (Pretraga, search).

What the portal shows for this number: “Број предмета: Ст 275/2026”, “Датум пријема: 02/11/2026” (date received), “Основ спора: ПРЕДЛОГ ЗА ПОКРЕТАЊЕ СТЕЧАЈНОГ ПОСТУПКА” (petition to open bankruptcy). In the “Радња” table (court actions):

- 02/11/2026 — “Дана 02/11/2026 примљен је предмет Ст-275/2026”: case Ст-275/2026 was received by the court on 02.11.2026.
- 03/11/2026 — “РЕШЕЊЕ О ПОКРЕТАЊУ СТЕЧАЈНОГ ПОСТУПКА (ОТВАРАЊУ) … датум експедовања: 03/11/2026”: decision opening bankruptcy, issued and sent on 03.11.2026 (“датум експедовања” is the date the document was sent).
- 03/11/2026 — “ДОПИС-ОСТАЛО … ОГЛАС О ОТВАРАЊУ СТЕЧАЈНОГ ПОСТУПКА”: other correspondence, the notice of the opening of bankruptcy, sent on 03.11.2026. The notice is expected in the Official Gazette; the claim deadline runs from its publication.

“Статус: НИЈЕ РЕШЕН” (not resolved) means the case is still running, not that the court has decided nothing. New court steps will appear in the same table.


The decision itself, naming the bank, is posted on the court's [electronic notice board](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/index.php?prf=con&id=56) (section “Rešenje o pokretanju stečajnog postupka (otvaranju)”, “Preuzmite” in the case row) and published in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik). The legal effects of bankruptcy start on the day the notice is posted on the board ([art. 9](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)).

### 2. When to file the claim

You file the claim for the amount above €50,000 in writing with the bankruptcy court. You have 90 days. The count starts when the bankruptcy notice appears in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik) (court decision of 03.11.2026, 10 St 275/2026, item IV). Until the notice appears, the deadline does not run. Don't wait for the €50,000 payout: the deadline runs regardless.

<small>More: [claim above €50,000](/en/trebovanie/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 3. What to expect

How much the bank owes depositors above the limit will be clear after the claims review: AOD has up to 6 months after the filing period ends, then the court holds a hearing no later than 60 days after that ([Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks, art. 17](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). There is no payout deadline: money is paid as the bank's assets are sold. At Univerzal banka (2014) the first payment above the limit came after 6 years 7 months (09.2020). Once payouts above the limit begin, send AOD your payment details: at Univerzal, money was not transferred without them.

<small>More: [how it went with Univerzal](/en/kak-bylo/)</small>

</div>

**Example: you have €140,000 at ALTA.** This is an example, not a forecast.

- **€50,000** is paid by AOD; you don't file anything in court for it.
- **€90,000** is your claim in court, filed within the 90 days the court set, counted from publication of the notice. The money comes as the bank's assets are sold. At Univerzal the court distributed 50%, 40% and 10% to rank I after 6 years 7 months, 7 years 2 months and 7 years 4 months, with no interest for the years of waiting.

If you have less than €50,000, AOD repays all of it: you don't file anything in court; watch for announcements on [aod.rs](https://www.aod.rs/).

Why the licence was revoked, in the National Bank's words: the bank's financial condition "deteriorated significantly", threatening the interests of its depositors ([full quote](/en/bank/#why)).

## Up to €50,000: AOD pays

This part comes from the state deposit insurance fund, and you don't file anything in court. The paying bank is Banka Poštanska štedionica (AOD, 03.11.2026). It signed the payout contract with AOD on 05.11. AOD will announce the start date and branches on aod.rs. See the [official AOD announcement](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/137) and the [AOD questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori).
