For ALTA depositors with more than €50,000: AOD repays up to €50,000; the rest comes back only through the bank's bankruptcy, on a claim you file yourself.

## Three steps if you have more than €50,000

<div class="steps3">
<div>

### 1. <span class="today">Today: save statements and screenshots from ALTA web banking while it still opens</span>

- PDF statements for every account;
- a screenshot of the web banking home page ("Početna") with the date visible;
- your account and deposit agreements.

You don't need to file anything yet.

<small>How to export them: [What to save](/en/vypiski/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 2. When to file the claim

You file the claim for the amount above €50,000 in writing with the bankruptcy court. The court sets the deadline: 30 to 90 days from the day its notice appears in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik). Don't wait for the €50,000 payout first: the deadline runs regardless.

<small>More: [claim above €50,000](/en/trebovanie/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 3. What to expect

About 8 to 12 months after bankruptcy opens, it should be clear how much the bank owes depositors above the limit. There is no payout deadline: money is paid as the bank's assets are sold. At Univerzal banka (2014) the first payment above the limit came after 6 years 7 months (09.2020). Once payouts above the limit begin, send AOD your payment details: at Univerzal, money was not transferred without them.

<small>More: [how it went with Univerzal](/en/kak-bylo/)</small>

</div>
</div>

<div class="example">

**Example: you have €140,000 at ALTA.** This is an example, not a forecast.

- **€50,000** is paid by AOD; you don't file anything in court for it.
- **€90,000** is your claim in court, filed within the 30 to 90 days the court sets. The money comes as the bank's assets are sold. At Univerzal the court distributed 50%, 40% and 10% to rank I after 6 years 7 months, 7 years 2 months and 7 years 4 months, with no interest for the years of waiting.

</div>

If you have less than €50,000, AOD repays all of it: you don't file anything in court; watch for announcements on [aod.rs](https://www.aod.rs/).

Why the licence was revoked, in the National Bank's words: the bank's financial condition "deteriorated significantly", threatening the interests of its depositors ([full quote](/en/bank/#why)).

<p class="safe-strip not-content"><strong>Beware of scams.</strong> Do not send your passport or account details to anyone who promises to speed up the payout or buy your claim. Official announcements come from AOD (aod.rs), the National Bank (nbs.rs) and the Commercial Court in Belgrade; the bankruptcy notice is published in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik). <a href="/en/bezopasnost/">How to avoid scams →</a></p>

## Up to €50,000: AOD pays

This part comes from the state deposit insurance fund, and you don't file anything in court: the paying bank is Banka Poštanska štedionica (03.11.2026); AOD will announce the payout start date and branches by 6 November. See the [official AOD announcement](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/137) and the [AOD questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori).
