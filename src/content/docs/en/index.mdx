## What happens after the court decision

The court opened ALTA's bankruptcy on 03.11.2026. What comes next, by law and by how AOD handled Univerzal banka in 2014:

<div class="next-steps">

1. **AOD has announced the date: payout of up to €50,000 from 13.11.2026.** Per AOD's notice of 06.11.2026 ([PDF](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)), Banka Poštanska štedionica pays at the branches designated for ALTA depositors, during normal working hours. On 06.11 AOD published the [list of branches](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Spisak.pdf). The list has 54 branches in 13 cities. One of them, in Belgrade (Kraljice Marije 3), is marked “specialised, for payout only” (SPECIJALIZOVANA EKSPOZITURA SAMO ZA ISPLATU). What this mark means is not clear from the AOD document; we are waiting for clarification. Addresses and hours: [«How to get the insured amount»](/en/vyplata/#otdeleniya).
2. **Up to €50,000: at a Poštanska branch, until 03.11.2029.** Dinar deposits are paid in dinars, foreign-currency deposits in euros at the middle rate of 03.11.2026 ([AOD, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)). Bring a valid identity document (foreigners: passport) and the original deposit document: contract, savings book or account card. Sole traders and companies are paid only by transfer to an account in Serbia; they also need the OP form and, if someone other than the registered representative comes, an authorisation on the company letterhead. If someone else goes for an individual: an authorisation certified by a notary, dated no earlier than the day the licence was revoked (30.10.2026); authorisations issued before the revocation cannot be used ([AOD, forms](https://www.aod.rs/clan/altabanka/obrasci_altabanka)). If you disagree with the amount, file a complaint with AOD or Poštanska. Checklist for individuals, sole traders and companies: [«How to get the insured amount up to €50,000»](/en/vyplata/#chto-vzyat).
3. **Above €50,000: a claim to the court, 90 days.** The 90 days run from the day the notice appears in the Official Gazette, not from 3 November. The court decision says so (10 St 275/2026, item IV), and so does the law ([Law on Bankruptcy of Banks, art. 7](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). File it in writing with Privredni sud u Beogradu, Masarikova 2 ([court website](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/)). You can write it on [AOD's template](/en/trebovanie/#obrazac). [Where to find the notice](/en/trebovanie/#glasnik). More: [“Claim”](/en/trebovanie/).
4. **A loan with ALTA: keep paying; a safe-deposit box: wait for AOD.** What clients owe the bank is part of the bank's estate, which the administrator collects ([Bankruptcy Law, art. 101](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2009/104/25/reg)). In 2014, three days after the court decision, AOD published the account for Univerzal banka loan payments and how box holders could collect their items ([AOD notice, 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20160830213118/http://aod.rs/pdf/2014-02-06-obavestenje-o-nacinu-izmirivanja.pdf)). For ALTA, AOD announced the account on 06.11: pay to the dinar account of ALTA in bankruptcy held at the NBS, no. 780-282-42 and enter the loan account number in the «poziv na broj» field ([AOD instruction, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/uputstvo_novi_ra%C4%8Dun_6_11_2026.pdf)). If your loan is with ALTA GROUP DOO Beograd (not the bank): account 200-3408551901000-45 at Poštanska štedionica, loan account number in «poziv na broj»; these are two different legal entities, pay only to these two accounts ([NBS, 06.11.2026](https://www.nbs.rs/sr_RS/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21884)). Our tip: which of the two entities your loan is with is stated in your loan agreement.
5. **First months: inventory and claim review.** Within 60 days AOD takes an inventory of the bank's assets, then publishes a quarterly report on aod.rs (art. 16). AOD reviews claims for up to 6 months after the filing deadline, then within 60 days the court approves the list (art. 17). The creditors' committee has 5 members (exceptionally more, on a reasoned proposal by AOD), chosen by the judge on AOD's proposal, taking claim sizes into account; a bank bankruptcy has no creditors' assembly (art. 8 and 22).
6. **Payments above €50,000: by payment rank, no deadline.** First AOD recovers the insurance it paid out and the procedure costs, then rank I is paid: individuals, sole traders, micro, small and medium companies, all at the same percentage (art. 19–20 of the Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks; art. 54 para. 3 of the Bankruptcy Law). At Univerzal the first payment came after 6 years 7 months. More: [“Procedure”](/en/procedura/).

</div>

<div class="court-case">

**How to check the case at the court yourself.** Case number: **10 St 275/2026** (on the portal you enter Ст / 275 / 2026). Confirmation: the decision of the Business Registers Agency (APR) of 03.11.2026 entering the opening of ALTA's bankruptcy in the register; it cites the court decision opening bankruptcy “Посл.бр. 10 Ст. 275/2026 од 03.11.2026”.

**How to check in the APR register.** Open the [APR search](https://pretraga.apr.gov.rs/search) (“Oбjедињена претрага”), press “Матични број” (registration number), type 07074433 and press Enter. The result is “ALTA BANKA AD BEOGRAD - U STEČAJU”, status “У стечају” (in bankruptcy). Click the name: under “Законски заступници” (legal representatives) you see “РС - Агенција за осигурање депозита”, function “стечајни управник” (bankruptcy administrator); under “Одлуке регистратора” for 2026 there is the APR decision of 03.11.2026 (БДСЛ 18465/2026).

**How to follow the case on the courts portal:**

1. Open the portal [“Ток предмета привредних судова”](https://tpson.portal.sud.rs/tpprvs/) (Tok predmeta privrednih sudova, commercial court case progress). At the top left you can switch “Ћирилица | Latinica”.
2. “Суд” (Sud, court) → БЕОГРАД.
3. “Уписник” (Upisnik, case register) → Ст (St, bankruptcy).
4. “Број предмета” (Broj predmeta, case number) → 275.
5. “Година” (Godina, year) → 2026, then click another field, otherwise the year is reset.
6. Below the fields there is a picture with a sum, e.g. “389 + 96”: add it up and type the answer in the box under the picture.
7. Press “Претрага” (Pretraga, search).

What the portal shows for this number: “Број предмета: Ст 275/2026”, “Датум пријема: 02/11/2026” (date received), “Основ спора: ПРЕДЛОГ ЗА ПОКРЕТАЊЕ СТЕЧАЈНОГ ПОСТУПКА” (petition to open bankruptcy). In the “Радња” table (court actions):

- 02/11/2026 — “Дана 02/11/2026 примљен је предмет Ст-275/2026”: case Ст-275/2026 was received by the court on 02.11.2026.
- 03/11/2026 — “РЕШЕЊЕ О ПОКРЕТАЊУ СТЕЧАЈНОГ ПОСТУПКА (ОТВАРАЊУ) … датум експедовања: 03/11/2026”: decision opening bankruptcy, issued and sent on 03.11.2026 (“датум експедовања” is the date the document was sent).
- 03/11/2026 — “ДОПИС-ОСТАЛО … ОГЛАС О ОТВАРАЊУ СТЕЧАЈНОГ ПОСТУПКА”: other correspondence, the notice of the opening of bankruptcy, sent on 03.11.2026. The notice is expected in the Official Gazette; the claim deadline runs from its publication.

“Статус: НИЈЕ РЕШЕН” (not resolved) means the case is still running, not that the court has decided nothing. New court steps will appear in the same table.


The decision itself, naming the bank, is posted on the court's [electronic notice board](http://www.bg.pr.sud.rs/index.php?prf=con&id=56) (section “Rešenje o pokretanju stečajnog postupka (otvaranju)”, “Preuzmite” in the case row) and published in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik). The legal effects of bankruptcy start on the day the notice is posted on the board ([art. 9](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)).

### 2. When to file the claim

You file the claim for the amount above €50,000 in writing with the bankruptcy court. You have 90 days. The count starts when the bankruptcy notice appears in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik) (court decision of 03.11.2026, 10 St 275/2026, item IV). Until the notice appears, the deadline does not run. Don't wait for the €50,000 payout: the deadline runs regardless.

<small>More: [claim above €50,000](/en/trebovanie/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 3. What to expect

How much the bank owes depositors above the limit will be clear after the claims review: AOD has up to 6 months after the filing period ends, then the court holds a hearing no later than 60 days after that ([Law on Bankruptcy and Liquidation of Banks, art. 17](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2015/14/3/reg)). There is no payout deadline: money is paid as the bank's assets are sold. At Univerzal banka (2014) the first payment above the limit came after 6 years 7 months (09.2020). Once payouts above the limit begin, payment details will be needed: before the first distribution at Univerzal (2020), the bank in bankruptcy (administered by AOD) asked creditors to send a copy of their ID card, account number and phone.

<small>More: [how it went with Univerzal](/en/kak-bylo/)</small>

</div>
</div>

<div class="example">

**Example: you have €140,000 at ALTA.** This is an example, not a forecast.

- **€50,000** is paid by AOD; you don't file anything in court for it.
- **€90,000** is your claim in court, filed within the 90 days the court set, counted from publication of the notice. The money comes as the bank's assets are sold. At Univerzal the court distributed 50%, 40% and 10% to rank I after 6 years 7 months, 7 years 2 months and 7 years 4 months, with no interest for the years of waiting.

If you have less than €50,000, AOD repays all of it: you don't file anything in court; watch for announcements on [aod.rs](https://www.aod.rs/).

Why the licence was revoked, in the National Bank's words: the bank's financial condition "deteriorated significantly", threatening the interests of its depositors ([full quote](/en/bank/#why)).

<p class="safe-strip not-content"><strong>Beware of scams.</strong> Do not send your passport or account details to anyone who promises to speed up the payout or buy your claim. Official announcements come from AOD (aod.rs), the National Bank (nbs.rs) and the Commercial Court in Belgrade; the bankruptcy notice is published in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik). <a href="/en/bezopasnost/">How to avoid scams →</a></p>

## Up to €50,000: AOD pays

This part comes from the state deposit insurance fund, and you don't file anything in court. The paying bank is Banka Poštanska štedionica (AOD, 03.11.2026). It signed the payout contract with AOD on 05.11. The payout starts on 13.11.2026 and you can apply until 03.11.2029 ([AOD notice, 06.11.2026](https://www.aod.rs/uploads/dokumenti/altabanka/Obave%C5%A1tenje_o_po%C4%8Detku_isplate_OD_6nov2026.pdf)); what to bring: [checklist](/en/vyplata/#chto-vzyat). See the [official AOD announcement](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/137) and the [AOD questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori).
