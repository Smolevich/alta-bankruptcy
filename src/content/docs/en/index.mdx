The National Bank of Serbia revoked ALTA bank's licence on 30.10.2026. The state Deposit Insurance Agency (AOD) will repay up to €50,000 per person. Anything above that you get back only through the bank's bankruptcy. It is slow, and you must file the claim yourself. This site is for people who have more than €50,000 at ALTA.

## Three steps if you have more than €50,000

<div class="steps3">
<div>

### 1. What to do now

Keep everything that proves your balance: account and deposit agreements, account numbers, statements showing the balance, your passport. Watch [aod.rs](https://www.aod.rs/): AOD will post the payout announcement within 3 working days of the court's decision. You don't need to file anything before then. What exactly to keep and how to export statements: [What to save](/en/vypiski/).

<small>More: [what to prepare](/en/trebovanie/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 2. When to file the claim

You file the claim for the amount above €50,000 in writing with the bankruptcy court. The court sets the deadline: 30 to 90 days from the day its notice appears in the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik). Don't wait for the €50,000 payout first: the deadline runs regardless.

<small>More: [claim above €50,000](/en/trebovanie/)</small>

</div>
<div>

### 3. What to expect

About 8 to 12 months after bankruptcy opens, it should be clear how much the bank owes depositors above the limit. There is no payout deadline: money is paid as the bank's assets are sold. At Univerzal banka (2014) the first payment above the limit came after 6 years 7 months (09.2020). That is a guide, not a forecast: ALTA's timeline may differ.

<small>More: [how it went with Univerzal](/en/kak-bylo/)</small>

## Up to €50,000: AOD pays

This part comes from the state deposit insurance fund, and you don't file anything in court: AOD will announce the conditions, the paying bank and the dates. See the [official AOD announcement](https://www.aod.rs/clan/vest/vest_forma/137) and the [AOD questions and answers](https://www.aod.rs/clan/osigdepoz/pitanja_odgovori).
