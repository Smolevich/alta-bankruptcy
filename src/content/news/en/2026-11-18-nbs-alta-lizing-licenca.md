title: "National Bank revokes the licence of Alta lizing, the bank's leasing subsidiary"
date: 2026-11-18
publisher: nbs
order: 3
aside: "Not about deposits: this concerns a leasing company, not the bank"
summary: >-
  On 18.11.2026 the National Bank announced that its Executive Board decided on 17.11 to revoke the financial leasing licence of Alta lizing d.o.o. Beograd and found the conditions for liquidation met. Its sole owner is Alta banka. The NBS stresses that the decisions do not affect financial stability and that Alta lizing does not take deposits. Its assets exceed its liabilities, so it goes into liquidation, not bankruptcy; AOD is the liquidator. No new leasing contracts; existing ones stay in force and are paid as before: to account 105-0000003084086-19 at AIK banka, «позив на број» — the existing one or the contract number. The statement does not mention payouts to the bank's depositors. Quote: «Алта лизинг д.о.о. Београд није банка, већ друштво које се бави пословима финансијског лизинга и не прима депозите грађана и привреде.»
