title: "National Bank revokes the licence of Alta lizing, the bank's leasing subsidiary"

aside: "Not about deposits: this concerns a leasing company, not the bank"
summary: >-
  On 18.11.2026 the National Bank announced that its Executive Board decided on 17.11 to revoke the financial leasing licence of Alta lizing d.o.o. Beograd and found the conditions for liquidation met. The company is a subsidiary of Alta banka, which is its sole owner. The NBS stresses that the decisions do not affect the stability of the financial system and that Alta lizing is not a bank and does not take deposits from citizens or businesses. The company's assets exceed its liabilities, so it goes into liquidation rather than bankruptcy; AOD acts as liquidator. No new leasing contracts; existing ones stay in force. Our reading: this does not affect payouts to the bank's depositors; the NBS statement says nothing about them. Quote: «Алта лизинг д.о.о. Београд није банка, већ друштво које се бави пословима финансијског лизинга и не прима депозите грађана и привреде.»
