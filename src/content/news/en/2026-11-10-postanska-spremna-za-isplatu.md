  On 10.11.2026 Poštanska štedionica announced: payout starts 13.11.2026, the appointment portal opens 12.11.2026. Payout points: the former network of 19 ALTA branches plus additional ones; 31 branches in Belgrade and a new specialised one at Kraljice Marije 3 (28 windows, only for this payout); Novi Sad 7 branches, Niš 3, and Sombor. Bring an ID card or passport and the original document on the ALTA account or deposit; entrepreneurs and companies also need the representative's ID and the OP form. Details on branches, hours and documents “soon”.
