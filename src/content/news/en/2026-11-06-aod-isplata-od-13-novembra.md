title: "AOD: payout of up to €50,000 starts on 13.11.2026 at Poštanska štedionica"

publisher: aod
order: 5
forms: [pravna]
summary: >-
  On 06.11.2026 AOD announced that the payout of insured ALTA deposits starts on 13.11.2026 through Banka Poštanska štedionica: up to €50,000 per depositor. Dinar deposits are paid in dinars, foreign-currency deposits in euros at the middle rate of 03.11.2026. How applications will be taken is not yet announced: there is no branch list (aod.rs, posted.co.rs, checked 06.11), so where to go is unknown. Only the start date is known: 13 November. Before then AOD is to publish the branch list; further notices are likely; we are watching and will update the site. Bring an ID and the original deposit document; sole traders and companies also the OP form and, if not the registered representative, an authorisation on letterhead. Deadline: 03.11.2029. Quotes: «…исплата осигураних износа депозита почиње дана 13.11.2026. године преко Банке Поштанска штедионица…»; «…најкасније до 3.11.2029. године.»
