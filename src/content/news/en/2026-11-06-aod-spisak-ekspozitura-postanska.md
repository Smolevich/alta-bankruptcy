title: "AOD publishes the list of Poštanska branches for the ALTA payout"
date: 2026-11-06
publisher: aod
order: 6
summary: >-
  On 06.11.2026 AOD posted the "List of Banka Poštanska štedionica branches for collecting the insured amount": 54 branches in 13 cities (32 in Belgrade, 7 in Novi Sad, 2 each in Niš, Kragujevac, Kraljevo and Užice, one each in Vršac, Lučani, Novi Pazar, Stara Pazova, Subotica, Čačak and Šabac). One of them, AOD - Centrala banke, Kraljice Marije 3, Belgrade (weekdays 08:00–19:30, Saturday 08:00–13:00), is marked “specialised, for payout only” (SPECIJALIZOVANA EKSPOZITURA SAMO ZA ISPLATU). What this mark means is not clear from the AOD document; we are waiting for clarification. Addresses, hours and map links are on "How to get the insured amount up to €50,000", section "Poštanska branches for the payout" (/en/vyplata/#postanska-branches). The list has no phone numbers. The payout starts on 13.11.2026.
outdated:
  note: "On 11.11.2026 AOD replaced the list: it now has 57 payout locations in 15 cities (Kruševac, Sombor and a third location in Niš were added). On 10.11.2026 Poštanska explained that Kraljice Marije 3 is a new specialised branch at the bank's head office, with 28 counters, only for this payout."
