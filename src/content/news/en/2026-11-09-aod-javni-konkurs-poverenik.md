title: "AOD announces a competition for its trustee in the ALTA banka bankruptcy"
date: 2026-11-09
publisher: aod
order: 2
summary: >-
  On 09.11.2026 AOD announced a public competition to appoint its trustee (poverenik) in the bankruptcy of ALTA banka a.d. Beograd u stečaju. Basis: Article 9 of the Law on AOD and Management Board decision 241/26 of 06.11.2026. AOD is the bankruptcy administrator by decision of the Commercial Court in Belgrade 10 СТ 275/2026 of 03.11.2026. The trustee is chosen from the list of licensed bankruptcy administrators and runs the proceedings to the end. Applications: 7 days from publication in “Politika”. Nothing changes for depositors: the claim filing deadline and the payout of the insured amount are unchanged. Key quote in Serbian (Cyrillic): «Агенција за осигурање депозита покреће поступак именовања повереника са листе лиценцираних стечајних управника ради спровођења и окончања стечаја над стечајним дужником АЛТА банком а.д. Београд у стечају.»
