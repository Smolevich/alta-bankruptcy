title: "AOD seeks an audit firm to review ALTA's financial report and the opening bankruptcy balance"
date: 2026-11-26
publisher: aod
order: 1
summary: >-
  On 26.11.2026 AOD, as bankruptcy administrator of ALTA, invited audit firms to independently review the bank's extraordinary financial report and the drawing up of the opening bankruptcy balance. The firm will check the books, the inventory and valuation of assets and liabilities, material adjustments and the bank's transactions in the 6 months before bankruptcy; AOD draws up the reports and stays responsible. Offers are due by 04.12.2026, 16:00. The document does not state the date the report is made up to. For depositors: the opening balance is the first official list of what creditors will be paid from, including amounts above €50,000; under the court decision of 03.11.2026 AOD draws it up within 60 days of the legal effects of opening bankruptcy. Nothing to file. AOD quote: «услуге надзора над израдом почетног стечајног биланса Стечајног дужника»
