summary: >-
  On 18.11.2026 AOD published a notice on how to get certificates from ALTA banka in bankruptcy. There are three: a certificate of the validity of the claim (if you have no original or valid document for the payout), one for unused cheques, and a certificate of validity of an authorisation to dispose of the funds (for authorisations before 3.11.2026). Apply in person at Bulevar Zorana Đinđića 121, Novi Beograd, on weekdays 9:00-15:00; by registered post to the same address (11070 Novi Beograd) or by e-mail to potvrde@altabanka.rs, the last two only for depositors outside Belgrade. The certificate comes back by post or as a scan to hand in at Poštanska. Cheques go back to ALTA; for invalid ones attach the Official Gazette notice. The notice gives no deadline and no fee. Quote: «Захтеви се могу слати на мејл адресу potvrde@altabanka.rs»
