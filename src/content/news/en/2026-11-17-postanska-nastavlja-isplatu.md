  On 17.11.2026 Poštanska štedionica reported that by 16.11 it had paid out €32,841,533.39 and RSD 1,979,267,749.20, which is 14.71% of the euro amount and 14.41% of the dinar amount due to be paid. 20,758 clients have booked an appointment. Only clients with an appointment are paid. Book on the bank's portal; those who cannot do it themselves can book through the contact centre on 011 20 20 292 or with the help of branch staff.
