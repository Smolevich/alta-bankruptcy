---
title: "National Bank warns of fraudulent SMS from \"AltaPay\": do not pay on them"
date: 2026-11-04
publisher: nbs
summary: >-
  The National Bank received reports from citizens of SMS messages with the sender name "AltaPay", and of SMS from unknown numbers, calling ALTA borrowers to pay their loans to given accounts at Poštanska štedionica bank. NBS asks clients of Alta banka in bankruptcy and Alta Group not to pay on the basis of such SMS or similar emails. Borrowers will receive clear instructions in good time only from the Deposit Insurance Agency, which has taken over management of the bank; until then they should wait for its notice naming the bank and account number. NBS immediately informed Poštanska štedionica so that it applies enhanced control of suspicious transactions and inflows on the account named in one of the SMS. Quote: "da nikako ne vrše uplate na osnovu ovakvih ili sličnih SMS poruka".
