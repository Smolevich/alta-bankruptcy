summary: >-
  According to Insajder on 11.11.2026, the Prosecutor's Office for Organised Crime has barred ALTA's owner Davor Macura and some of his companies from disposing of assets (its journalists saw the documents). «Tužilaštvo za organizovani kriminal je u okviru istrage koju vodi protiv Macure donelo naredbu o zabrani raspolaganja imovinom koju je stekao kupovinom IMT-a.» The orders cover about 35 ha of land of the former IMT factory in New Belgrade (part of IMT's property was bought in 2021 by Macura's company ABL Solvent for about €70m), land of Alta holding and Alta lizing in New Belgrade and Barajevo, land and a residential building of Campari International, and his own property in Senjak, Zemun Polje, Sopot and garages in New Belgrade. This is a ban on disposal, not a confiscation or a decision on depositor payouts; law professor Goran Ilić says a court will decide on temporary seizure.
