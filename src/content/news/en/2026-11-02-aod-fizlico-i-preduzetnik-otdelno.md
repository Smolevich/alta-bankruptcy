---
title: "AOD in writing: the €50,000 limit applies separately to an individual and to the same person as a sole trader"
date: 2026-11-02
publisher: aod
order: 1
summary: >-
  A depositor asked AOD in writing; AOD replied on 02.11.2026. Personal and sole-trader (preduzetnik) accounts are not added up: "limit osiguranja depozita od 50.000 evra primenjuje se zasebno na fizičko lice i na isto to lice registrovano kao preduzetnik". Individuals are paid in cash or by transfer; sole traders and companies by transfer only, to the account they name (AOD procedure, section VIII item 31). Transfers go only to an account at a bank in Serbia, not abroad: "prenos na račun u banci u Srbiji". Whether a sole trader is paid to the business or a personal account AOD has not yet said; the question has been asked. Screenshots of the letters are on the Claim page.
