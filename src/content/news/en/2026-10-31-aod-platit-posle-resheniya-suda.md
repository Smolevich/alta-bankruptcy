summary: "Payouts start within 7 working days of the Commercial Court in Belgrade opening bankruptcy; AOD will announce the conditions, places and dates within 3 working days of that decision. The limit is €50,000 per depositor: dinar deposits are paid in dinars, foreign-currency deposits in euros at the middle exchange rate on the day bankruptcy is opened. Individuals, sole traders and micro, small and medium-sized companies are entitled. Separately, AOD promises an appeal to small investors (securities, brokerage accounts). (Our note: nothing needs to be filed before the AOD announcement.) AOD dates the National Bank's decision 31.10; the National Bank itself gives 30.10."
