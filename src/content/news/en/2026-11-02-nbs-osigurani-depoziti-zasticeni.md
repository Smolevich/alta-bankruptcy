---
title: "National Bank: \"Insured deposits are protected, the banking sector is stable\""
date: 2026-11-02
publisher: nbs
summary: >-
  The National Bank's Executive Board says the banking sector is stable, well capitalised and highly liquid, and that one bank's problems must not be equated with the sector as a whole. All deposits covered by deposit insurance will be paid out in the full insured amount: up to €50,000 per depositor, with the payout guaranteed by the Republic of Serbia. The Deposit Insurance Agency has the funds and will pay within the statutory deadline. Depositors will be told "in a timely manner" which bank will pay and what the procedures are; the statement names neither the bank nor dates. The National Bank also commits to keeping the dinar stable against the euro, including through foreign-exchange interventions. Key sentence (Serbian original): "svi depoziti obuhvaćeni zakonskim sistemom osiguranja depozita zaštićeni i da će biti isplaćeni u punom osiguranom iznosu".
