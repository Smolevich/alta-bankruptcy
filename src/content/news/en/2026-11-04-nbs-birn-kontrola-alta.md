title: "National Bank answers BIRN: part of the questions on ALTA supervision is confidential; licence revoked to protect depositors"
date: 2026-11-04
publisher: nbs
summary: >-
  BIRN put 12 questions to the National Bank about ALTA's supervision and operations. Questions 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 11 and 12 (inspections, steps on illiquidity, cash in the vault, largest depositors, anti-money-laundering) went unanswered: by law, data on the supervision of an individual bank is confidential. Answers given: in 2026 ALTA neither requested nor received liquidity support or credit relief from NBS; systemically important banks are identified under European Banking Authority criteria at least once a year; whether the cash was ever in the vault and whether the reports were false is for the prosecutor and police; revoking the licence, to protect depositors and financial stability, required a rapid reaction. Quote: "ALTA banka ad Beograd u 2026. godini nije tražila, niti dobila bilo koju vrstu podrške likvidnosti".
