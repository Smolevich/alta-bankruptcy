summary: >-
  On 27.11.2026 AOD published an infographic, "Payout of insured amounts in numbers", on its Alta banka page. The Deposit Insurance Fund transferred €380,375,464 (total converted to euros) to Poštanska štedionica for payouts: €250,880,000 and RSD 15,224,755,828. 67,443 depositors are entitled to the insured amount: 53,720 individuals, 8,646 entrepreneurs, 3,429 micro, 1,262 small and 386 medium legal entities. As of 20 November the Fund had €917,289,543 left (dinar and foreign-currency funds converted to euros). Quote: «Укупно пренето Банци Поштанска штедионица из Фонда за осигурање депозита за потребе исплате осигураних износа депозита: 380.375.464 евра».
