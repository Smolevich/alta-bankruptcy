title: "National Bank revokes ALTA GROUP licence: it is not a bank, ALTA banka deposits are not affected"
date: 2026-11-02
publisher: nbs
summary: >-
  ALTA GROUP DOO BEOGRAD is a separate company (e-money and payment services: wallets, payment orders, cards), not a bank. On 2 November the National Bank revoked its licence, automatically, after the licence of ALTA banka was revoked. Bank deposits are not affected: they go through the bank's bankruptcy procedure and the AOD payout is unchanged. The National Bank says payment-service users' funds are protected. ALTA GROUP clients can use other banks and payment institutions in Serbia; ALTA GROUP's debtors still owe and will get instructions. Quote (Serbian): "ALTA GROUP DOO BEOGRAD nije banka i ne prima depozite građana i privrede".
