title: "Poštanska asks the National Bank for additional supervision; the bank is “envisaged” as the ALTA paying bank"
date: 2026-11-02
publisher: postanska
summary: >-
  On 02.11.2026 Banka Poštanska štedionica published a press release: on Friday morning the bank asked the National Bank for additional expert supervision of its liquidity and financial flows, because of negative news and insinuations questioning its operations and liquidity. The bank writes that it is envisaged as the paying bank for ALTA's insured deposits, while the procedure for adopting the decision on this engagement is still under way. According to the bank, the National Bank's measures are enhanced liquidity monitoring and additional procedures for certain transactions. Quote: «…Банка Поштанска штедионица предвиђена за банку исплатиоца осигураних депозита Алта банке, док је поступак доношења одговарајућег решења о том ангажовању још у току.»
