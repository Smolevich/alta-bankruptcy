summary: >-
  On 06.11.2026 AOD published a call to small investors who were ALTA investment-services clients (brokerage account, securities, money on an investment account). Ordinary depositors are not affected: deposits are paid by Poštanska from 13.11. The Investor Protection Fund pays investors up to €20,000 in dinars at the NBS middle rate of 03.11.2026. Apply on AOD's form by post or at AOD's registry office (Masarikova 5, Belgrade) by 03.05.2027 (for a justified reason, by 03.11.2027 with evidence). Attach originals or certified copies of contracts and confirmations; individuals a copy of ID, legal entities an APR extract. AOD draws up the list of recognised claims within 60 days of 03.11.2026; objections within 10 days. Contact: 011/7358 659, zastita.investitora@aod.rs. Quote: «…најкасније до 3.5.2027. године поштом или непосредно путем писарнице…»
