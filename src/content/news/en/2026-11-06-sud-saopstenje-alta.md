summary: >-
  On 06.11.2026 the Commercial Court in Belgrade published a press release. The court received the National Bank's decision on 02.11.2026 and the next day, 03.11.2026, issued decision St 275/2026 opening bankruptcy of ALTA banka. The same day the decision was published on the court notice board; the notice of the opening of bankruptcy was published on the court notice board and electronic notice board and sent for publication in the daily Danas and the Official Gazette of the Republic of Serbia. The legal consequences of opening bankruptcy took effect on the day the notice was posted on the court notice board, 03.11.2026. AOD is the bankruptcy administrator. The court says it will inform the public in a timely manner about the further course of the proceedings.
