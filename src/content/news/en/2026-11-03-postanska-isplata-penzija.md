title: "Poštanska: one-off pension payout to ALTA clients at all branches"
date: 2026-11-03
publisher: postanska
summary: >-
  On 03.11.2026 Banka Poštanska štedionica started a one-off payout of pensions to people who received their pension through Alta banka. Schedule: 03.11 for self-employed pensioners, 05.11 for farmers and military pensioners, 10.11 for former employees. All Poštanska branches pay; a valid ID card (lična karta) is needed. The bank writes that the one-off payout does not oblige anyone to move their pension to Poštanska for good. This is not the payout of insured deposits up to €50,000: AOD will announce that date and the branches. Quote in Serbian (Cyrillic): «почела је данас једнократну исплату пензија корисницима из категорије самосталних делатности који су пензију до сада примали преко Алта банке».
