summary: >-
  On 6 November the National Bank announced that it revoked the authorisation of the authorised exchange office “Alta Group d.o.o. Beograd” to carry out exchange operations and imposed fines totalling RSD 250 million on the legal entity and the responsible person in it. The inspection found breaches of the Law on Foreign Exchange Operations and its by-laws, and a flagrant breach of the anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing rules. The findings go to the Organised Crime Prosecutor's Office, the Anti-Money-Laundering Administration and, if needed, other authorities. The release does not link the exchange office to ALTA banka and says nothing about deposits or the exchange office's clients. The name matches the company that this site shows as the bank's sole shareholder (page “Bank”); the release does not confirm they are the same entity.
