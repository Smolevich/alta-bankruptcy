title: "National Bank: payments left at exchange offices were mostly executed; the rest the same day"
date: 2026-11-04
publisher: nbs
summary: >-
  The National Bank answered whether citizens' payments (bills, transfers) left at exchange offices that worked through ALTA went through. The offices were agents of ALTA GROUP DOO BEOGRAD, whose licence NBS revoked on 2 November; on 30 October NBS imposed a temporary measure to protect the money received. Before the revocation most orders were executed; the rest were expected on 4 November. Our note: if a payment made at an exchange office before 30 October did not arrive, contact the office and NBS; no new application is needed. The offices may sign with any other payment institution or Pošta Srbije. Euros: in October they sold €744.9m and bought €646.1m, and banks supplied cash without interruption. Quote: "ова институција електронског новца извршила је већину примљених налога корисника платних услуга".
