title: "National Bank: payments left at exchange offices were mostly executed; the rest the same day"
date: 2026-11-04
publisher: nbs
summary: >-
  The National Bank answered whether citizens' payments (bills, transfers) left at exchange offices that worked through ALTA went through. The offices were agents of ALTA GROUP DOO BEOGRAD, whose licence NBS revoked on 2 November; on 30 October NBS imposed a temporary measure to protect the money received. Before the revocation most orders were executed; the rest were expected on 4 November. The offices may sign with any other payment institution or Pošta Srbije. From 1 October to 2 November exchange offices sold €744.9m and bought €646.1m; banks supplied them with cash without interruption. Quote: "ova institucija elektronskog novca izvršila je većinu primljenih naloga korisnika platnih usluga".
