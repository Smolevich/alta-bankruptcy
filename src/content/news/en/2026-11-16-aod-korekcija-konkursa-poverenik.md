title: "AOD corrects the trustee competition: applications due 20.11 by 16:00"
date: 2026-11-16
publisher: aod
order: 2
summary: >-
  On 16.11.2026 AOD corrected its 09.11 competition for its trustee (poverenik) in the ALTA banka bankruptcy. The trustee is not a depositors' representative but a licensed administrator appointed by AOD. The correction changes the "Application" section: instead of "7 days from publication in “Politika”" the deadline is Friday 20.11.2026, 16:00; applications go to the AOD registry office (Masarikova 5, 11th floor). Concerns candidates only. Depositors need do nothing: the claim deadline and payout are unchanged. The original competition is in the 09.11 news item. Quote (Cyrillic): «Пријава на јавни конкурс ће се сматрати благовременом ако је примљена на писарници Агенције до петка 20.11.2026.г до 16 часова по локалном времену.»
