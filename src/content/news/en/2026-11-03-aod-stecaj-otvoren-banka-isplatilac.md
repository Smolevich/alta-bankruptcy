title: "The court opened bankruptcy of ALTA banka; AOD is the administrator, the paying bank is Poštanska štedionica"
date: 2026-11-03
publisher: aod
summary: >-
  The court opened bankruptcy of ALTA banka on 03.11.2026; AOD is the administrator and the paying bank is Banka Poštanska štedionica. By 6.11 AOD will announce the payout start date and branches. Payouts must start within 7 working days; money can be collected within 3 years. Individuals, sole traders and small businesses are protected up to €50,000: dinars paid in dinars, foreign currency in euros. Original in Cyrillic, here in Latin: “Privredni sud u Beogradu danas je doneo rešenje o pokretanju stečajnog postupka nad Alta bankom, kojim je Agencija za osiguranje depozita imenovana za stečajnog upravnika. … Agencija za osiguranje depozita dužna je da započne isplatu osiguranih iznosa depozita u roku od narednih sedam radnih dana. … Dinarski ulozi će se isplaćivati u dinarima, a devizni u evrima.”
