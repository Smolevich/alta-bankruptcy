---
title: "The court opened bankruptcy of ALTA banka; AOD is the administrator, the paying bank is Poštanska štedionica"
date: 2026-11-03
publisher: aod
summary: >-
  The court opened bankruptcy of ALTA banka on 03.11.2026; AOD is the administrator and the paying bank is Banka Poštanska štedionica. By 6.11 AOD will announce the payout start date and branches. Payouts must start within 7 working days; money can be collected within 3 years. Individuals, sole traders and small businesses are protected up to €50,000: dinars paid in dinars, foreign currency in euros. Case no. 10 St 275/2026, confirmed by the APR register (“ALTA BANKA AD BEOGRAD - U STEČAJU”). AOD original in English: “Today the Commercial Court of Belgrade has initiated the bankruptcy process in Alta banka, appointing the Deposit Insurance Agency (DIA) as the bankruptcy administrator. By Friday 6 November 2026, the DIA will announce… the start date of reimbursement… Legal deadline for the DIA to initiate the reimbursement process is seven working days starting from today.”
