summary: >-
  On 06.11.2026 the National Bank of Serbia explained to borrowers of ALTA banka a.d. Beograd in bankruptcy and of ALTA GROUP DOO Beograd how to keep settling their obligations to them. These are two different legal entities, currently in a different legal status, so obligations to them (for example, under loan agreements) are settled to different accounts. ALTA banka in bankruptcy: the dinar account at the National Bank of Serbia 780-282-42. Borrowers who had loans with ALTA GROUP DOO Beograd keep paying to account 200-3408551901000-45 at Poštanska štedionica ad. In both cases the loan account number (broj partije kredita) goes in the «poziv na broj» field. The NBS repeated its warning: pay only to these two accounts and do not trust suspicious messages or emails directing you to pay to other accounts.
