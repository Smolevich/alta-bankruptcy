title: "AOD: contact centre of ALTA banka in bankruptcy opens — for loan obligations"

publisher: aod

summary: >-
  On 10.11.2026 AOD announced that the contact centre of ALTA banka in bankruptcy, from that day, gives clients the information they need about their loan obligations. Citizens and legal entities can call the operators on 011/220-5500, every working day from 9:00 to 16:00. Quote: «Контакт-центар Алта банке а.д. Београд у стечају од данас пружа корисницима потребне информације у вези с њиховим кредитним обавезама.» The notice speaks only of loan obligations; it says nothing about paying out the insured amount or about claims filed with the court.
