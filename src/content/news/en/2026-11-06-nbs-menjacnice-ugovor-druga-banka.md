title: "National Bank: what exchange offices that worked with ALTA must do after signing with another bank"
date: 2026-11-06
publisher: nbs
summary: >-
  The National Bank set out the procedure for authorised exchange offices that had a contract with Alta banka (licence revoked 30 October). After signing with another commercial bank, they must sell it the cash currency bought from ALTA before 30 October, within 15 or 30 days of purchase, and provide documents proving the purchase from ALTA and the amount. Offices that worked only with ALTA sell the bank at least 80% of last week's net cash purchases, also once the contract with another bank is in place. For ordinary people nothing changes: no action is needed. For the offices, see the 4 November item. Quote: "по успостављању уговорног односа са том другом банком".
