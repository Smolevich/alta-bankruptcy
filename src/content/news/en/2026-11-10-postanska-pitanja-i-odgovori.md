  On 10.11.2026 Poštanska štedionica published 39 payout questions and answers. Requests can be filed until 03.11.2029, no need to come on day one (item 2). Besides ID, bring the original deposit document; if lost, a certificate or certified copy from ALTA in bankruptcy (items 14–15). Power of attorney: on the AOD form, given after 03.11.2026 and certified (item 16). Individuals choose cash or transfer; sole traders and companies get a transfer only, and only to the depositor's own account (items 22, 25). Personal and sole-trader accounts: separate limits (item 10). Interest counts to 03.11.2026; no need to wait for term deposits to end (item 9). Enquiries: 011/20-20-292, option 4. On 10.11 the «Онлајн заказивање» button led to a test form.
