  On 05.11.2026 Banka Poštanska štedionica and the Deposit Insurance Agency (AOD) signed a contract on paying out insured deposits to Alta banka depositors. In plain terms: Poštanska is now officially the bank that will hand out the money. The bank calls the signing a regular step in preparing the payout. The announcement gives no start date, conditions or procedure; AOD and Poštanska will announce them. Our note: nothing needs to be filed yet, wait for the official announcement.
