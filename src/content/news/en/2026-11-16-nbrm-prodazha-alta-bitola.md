summary: >-
  On 16.11.2026 the National Bank of North Macedonia announced the sale of the stake in ALTA banka AD Bitola: «Станува збор за 372.742 обични акции, односно 95,336% од вкупниот број акции, во сопственост на „Алта банка“ АД Белград – во стечај» (372,742 ordinary shares, 95.336% of all shares, owned by ALTA banka Beograd in bankruptcy). The sale runs on the Macedonian Stock Exchange as one block only («сè или ништо», all or nothing). First auction: 30.11.2026, starting price 3,777 Macedonian denars per share, about MKD 1.408 billion for the block, ≈€22.9 million at the NBRM rate for 16.11 (61.5037 denars per euro). If unsold, auctions follow on 1, 2, 3, 4 and 7.12, each 10% cheaper. Buyers must ask the NBRM by 19.11 for prior approval of a qualifying holding. The shares are ALTA's property in bankruptcy, so the proceeds go into the bankruptcy estate (Bankruptcy Law, art. 101).
