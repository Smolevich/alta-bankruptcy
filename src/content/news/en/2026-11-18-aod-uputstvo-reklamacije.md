summary: >-
  AOD has published instructions on complaints (reklamacija) by depositors of ALTA banka u stečaju. If you disagree with the insured-amount payout, you can file: (1) with ALTA banka u stečaju: in person at the counter, Bulevar Zorana Đinđića 121 (form on site, certified copy after handing in), at potvrde@altabanka.rs or by registered mail to the same address; (2) with AOD: reklamacijealta@aod.rs or by post, Masarikova 5, 11000 Beograd; (3) with Poštanska: in person at any ALTA payout branch (form and certified copy). Answer within 15 working days of receipt. The form is in the sources. AOD quote (Serbian): «Крајњи рок за одговор и решавање ваше рекламације је 15 радних дана од дана пријема од стране надлежне институције која оперативно обрађује рекламацију.»
