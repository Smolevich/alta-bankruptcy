title: "The cities of Vranje and Leskovac ask to exclude their budget funds from the ALTA bankruptcy estate"
date: 2026-11-07
publisher: city
order: 2
summary: >-
  Vranje and Leskovac state on their official websites that they are seeking the exclusion (izlučenje) of their budget funds from the bankruptcy estate. The money was held on overnight deposits in ALTA banka's account at the National Bank. Leskovac filed with the Commercial Court in Belgrade on 04.11.2026 (78,000,000 dinars) and also proposes an interim measure: nobody but the city may dispose of the money. Vranje (statement of 05.11.2026) names 177,000,000 dinars and says it has taken legal action before the court and AOD, including a request for exclusion; whether the claim is justified is decided in the procedure set by law. ALTA's bankruptcy case is 10 СТ 275/2026. Vranje's key quote in Serbian (Cyrillic): «предузете су правне радње ради заштите потраживања Града, укључујући захтев за излучење средстава»
