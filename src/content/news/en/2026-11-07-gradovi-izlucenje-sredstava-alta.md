title: "The cities of Vranje and Leskovac ask to exclude their budget funds from the ALTA bankruptcy estate"
date: 2026-11-07
publisher: city
order: 2
summary: >-
  Vranje and Leskovac state on their official websites that they have applied to the Commercial Court in Belgrade for the exclusion (izlučenje) of their budget funds from the bankruptcy estate. The money was held on overnight deposits in ALTA banka's account at the National Bank. Leskovac filed on 04.11.2026 (78,000,000 dinars) and also proposes an interim measure: nobody but the city may dispose of the money. Vranje (statement of 05.11.2026) names 177,000,000 dinars; whether the claim is justified is decided in the procedure set by law. For depositors this means: if the exclusion is granted, less remains in the common estate. ALTA's bankruptcy case is 10 СТ 275/2026. Vranje's key quote in Serbian (Cyrillic): «предузете су правне радње ради заштите потраживања Града, укључујући захтев за излучење средстава»
