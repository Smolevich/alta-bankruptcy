summary: >-
  On 27.11.2026 aod.rs published a notice from ALTA banka in bankruptcy to renters of personal safe deposit boxes at the Južni bulevar and Igumanova palata branches. To collect the contents, book first — from Monday 30.11.2026: by e-mail to sefovi@altabanka.rs (full name, JMBG, contact phone; the bank then contacts you to agree a time) or by phone 011/2205-690 on working days from 9:00 to 15:00. Overdue box rent must be paid before collection to account 780-282-42 (in «poziv na broj» — «JMBG-broj sefa», as worded in the notice), with proof of payment. A handover record is drawn up and the box rental contract is terminated. Quote: «Prijava i zakazivanje termina mogu se izvršiti na jedan od sledeća dva načina od ponedeljka 30.11.2026.godine».
