date: 2026-11-18
publisher: aod
order: 1
summary: >-
  On 18.11.2026 the AOD pages on Alta banka (announcements, forms, questions and answers) gained a link to «Образац рекламације», a one-page Word form. It is a complaint about the insured deposit amount as determined: the depositor states the grounds, what is attached, a phone number for the reply, name, address, ID number, JMBG or registration number and email. The form says the amount was presented to the depositor by Banka Poštanska štedionica, which pays the deposits. These AOD pages do not say where or by when to file it; our section on payouts has the procedure and the 15-working-day reply period. Quote from the form: «Овим улажем рекламацију на утврђену висину осигураног износа депозита.»
