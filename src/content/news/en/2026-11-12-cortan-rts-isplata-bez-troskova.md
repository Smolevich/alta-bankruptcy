title: "AOD director Čortan to RTS: transferring the insured payout to another bank is free"
date: 2026-11-12

summary: >-
  On 12.11.2026 AOD director Čortan told RTS that an individual can take the insured amount in cash or choose a transfer to their account at any other bank, practically electronically and without any costs. The payout funds have already been transferred to Poštanska; there is no need to rush, and the bank will provide cash at the chosen branch for everyone who has booked. Quote (Serbian): «Što se tiče fizičkih lica, svako će moći da podigne gotovinu. Moći će i da se opredeli da se sredstva prenesu na njegov račun u bilo kojoj drugoj banci praktično elektronski bez bilo kakvih troškova». These are the AOD director's words as reported by RTS, not an AOD document. The 0.3% tariff on the payout page applies to ordinary transfers from an account at Poštanska.
