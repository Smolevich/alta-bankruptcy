title: "AOD: Alta banka borrowers pay their loans to an NBS dinar account"
date: 2026-11-06
publisher: aod
order: 2
summary: >-
  On 06.11.2026 AOD published an instruction for Alta banka borrowers. Loans are not forgiven: after the bankruptcy was opened (decision of the Commercial Court in Belgrade 10 СТ 275/2026 of 03.11.2026), borrowers must keep paying under the terms of their loan agreements. Payments go to the dinar account at the National Bank of Serbia 780-282-42. In the payment slip field "poziv na broj" (reference number), enter the loan batch number (broj partije kredita). This concerns only people with a loan at ALTA; depositors are not affected. Key quote in Serbian (Cyrillic): «Уплате доспелих обавеза према Алта банци а.д. Београд у стечају могу се извршити на ДИНАРСКИ рачун код Народне банке Србије број 780-282-42. Потребно је у позиву на број навести број партије кредита.»
