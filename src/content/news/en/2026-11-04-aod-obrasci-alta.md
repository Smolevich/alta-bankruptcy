summary: >-
  On its “Alta banka u stečaju — Obrasci” page AOD published three forms. 1) “Образац пријаве потраживања”, a template for the claim above €50,000 to the Commercial Court in Belgrade: case number, creditor details (name, address, JMBG, account and bank; for a company, registration number and PIB), the debtor bank, the court decision and Official Gazette number, the amount on the day bankruptcy opened and its basis, the list of evidence, the request to recognise the claim, signature. Free form is also allowed if it contains the items of art. 111 of the Law on Bankruptcy (AOD). 2) and 3) Authorisation forms to collect the insured amount up to €50,000, one for individuals and one for companies and sole traders, in three copies. All forms have a JMBG field; they do not say how to fill it in if you have none. Copies and details: the “Claim” page, sections on the template and the authorisation.
