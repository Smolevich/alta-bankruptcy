title: "Alta lizing: court opens liquidation, AOD is the liquidator. Keep paying the lease as before, to the AIK banka account"

publisher: aod
order: 1
aside: "Not about deposits: only for clients of the leasing company"
summary: >-
  On 20.11.2026 the Belgrade Commercial Court (case 7. L 2/2026) opened liquidation of Alta Leasing, a subsidiary of Alta banka; AOD is the liquidator. The company takes no deposits; payouts to the bank's depositors are not affected. It concerns lessees: contracts stay in force, pay on schedule as before, to account 105-0000003084086-19 at AIK banka, with the existing approval or contract number as reference. Late payment means default interest, termination, repossession. After the last instalment and buyout the liquidator issues documents to remove the encumbrance in the APR. Creditors have 90 days from Official Gazette publication to file claims with the court. AOD quote: «Све уплате по основу уговора о финансијском лизингу врше се искључиво на рачун код АИК банке број: 105-0000003084086-19»
